What counts as a western blot. Western blot means proteins separated on a gel, transferred to a membrane and detected with an antibody. An OxyBlot counts. A dot blot or slot blot, of RNA or of protein, does not; neither does a capillary system such as Wes or Jess, which has no membrane, or a membrane probed only with streptavidin or a lectin, which uses no antibody. Items marked 'Western blot only' do not count the same thing described for another assay in the paper: qPCR, immunofluorescence, IHC, flow cytometry, ELISA, mass spectrometry, or a gel that was stained rather than blotted. Judge the text the page shows, including any figure legend printed in it; a detail found only in a legend or supplement that is not shown is not stated. A method given only by citation ('as previously described [12]') is not stated: mark only what the text itself says. The Shortcut citation item is the exception: it records the citation phrase itself. Some PDFs drop the micro sign or print it as m ('50 g', '25 mg' for micrograms); rule on the intended unit.

Every question carries its own What to mark rule with a worked example. Follow it rather than your instinct: consistency between reviewers matters more here than any single choice. Full rules under Guide.

EXPECTED OUTCOMES We describe a straightforward protocol for the generation of GOI knockout organoids. The gene- editing strategy we utilize here is based on transient expression of the editing complex, eliminating the need of establishing stably expressing Cas9 organoid cultures before being able to knockout the ll OPEN ACCESS 12 STAR Protocols 4, 102076, March 17, 2023 Protocol GOI.23 Importantly, this protocol is designed to minimize the risk of off-target gene editing, intro- duction of transcriptional variety, and potential immunogenetic effects associated with prolonged expression of the Cas9 protein.13-15 While we have not experimentally tested for off-target editing and target independent phenotype change frequencies, the transient presence of the editing com- plex and the Cas9 enzyme in the organoids in our method removes the main factors causing off- target alterations.12-17,24 Therefore, this protocol is well suited to generate syngeneic parental / GOI knockout organoid culture pairs, for the subsequent analysis of developmental, immunological and tumor biology processes either via in vitro or in vivo experiments. Possible assays include ge- netic, molecular, and biochemical characterization, organoid - immune cell co-culture assays or in vivo transplantation into mice to address numerous biological questions. While we demonstrate the feasibility of the protocol by successful establishment of four GOI knockouts using two different gastric cancer organoid cultures, we expect the protocol to be universally applicable to any robustly growing organoid culture of interest. The protocol includes a few critical steps at which efficiency of the process can be determined. For best results, starting cultures should be in the exponential growth phase (Figure 1). After steps 1-25 of the protocol, the organoids are to be single-cell sorted by fluorescent-activated cell sorting (FACS) for ATTO 550 positivity, as a surrogate marker for successful transfection of the editing com- plex. The FACS step allows quantification of the viability of the organoids post transfection (see gating strategy in Figure 2) and the efficacy of transfection (Table 1). In our hands, 32%-58% of disso- ciated organoid cells were viable, of which 30%-50% were highly positive for the ATTO 550 marker (Table 1). The outgrowth efficacy of organoids after cell sorting, represented by the percentage of organoids growing from the total number of wells of 96-well plates or the percentage of organoids growing from the total number of sorted cells, may widely differ. We introduce two supplementary methods to ensure that organoids are outgrowing. Either single cells are sorted in individual wells of 96-well plates (step 30) or single cells are pool/bulk sorted into a vial and plated into domes at a cell density of 2,500 cells per dome (steps 35-40). Method 1 ensures that outgrowing organoids are single cell derived clones but generate only a low number of organoid cultures with an outgrowth rate between 1%-3% (Table 2). In Method 2, many more cells can be seeded, and therefore a comparable outgrowth rate of 1%-8% leads to high numbers of organoids (Table 2). Subsequently, these can be manually picked (step 42, Figure 4, Methods Video S1), to establish or- ganoid cultures of clonal origin. As the final check of successful gene-editing of the GOI, we perform protein immunoblotting to confirm loss of target protein, an example is provided in Figure 5. For our example gene edits, we had 6%-40% of tested organoid cultures of clonal origin having complete loss of the target protein and 9%-40% a significantly reduced protein level compared to parental organoids (Table 3). At this high editing rate, it is very feasible to establish and utilize not only parental / complete knockout pairs of clonal organoid cultures for subsequent experimentation but also parental / complete / heterozygous loss mutant trios, if desired. LIMITATIONS This protocol is not suitable for CRISPR-Cas9-mediated high through-put screening efforts as the ed- iting efficacy is not sufficient and the organoid handling times are too high. For screening efforts typically viral delivered, permanent Cas9-expressing cell lines or organoid cultures are being used.23,25 Our protocol utilizes transient expression and activity of gene editing complexes. While our protocol has a lower editing efficacy compared to permanent Cas9 expression methods, the transient presence of the editing components is known to reduce off target editing and mitigates the risk of Cas9-caused phenotypical changes, such as increased immunogenicity.12-17,24 This protocol is suited to generate gene knockout organoids. If base -editing or gene knock-in ma- nipulations are desired this protocol may be adapted or alternative protocols, employing similar methodology, maybe used as described in the literature.2,7 This protocol and many other CRISPR-Cas9-editing protocols require robust growth potential of the organoids over several passages. Organoids which grow at a very slow rate, or which stop to prolif- erate after approximately five or less passages will not be suitable. This is because the outgrowth of clones after cell sorting and clonal expansion for GOI knockout confirmation by western blot requires continuous and robust organoid proliferation. The outgrowth efficacy of organoids from single cells sorted in individual wells of 96-well plates may differ strongly for different organoid cultures. The single cell environment does pose a stress on the cells and without the support from niche cells outgrowth capacity will be different for every organoid culture. To ensure that sufficient organoids are outgrowing we introduce a second supplementary method. Next to single cells sorted in individual wells of 96-well plates, single cells are pool/bulk sorted into a vial and plated into domes at a cell density of 2,500 cells per dome. With this method, many more cells can be seeded, and therefore a comparable outgrowth rate of 1%-8% leads to high numbers of organoids, which subsequently can be handpicked for organoid culture expansion.                         When a paper gives several

                        Many papers list a table of antibodies with eight RRIDs, eight catalog numbers and several vendors. Mark them all. Press Use highlighted text once per value and they stack up in a numbered list. Press Done, next when the list is complete.

                        If they sit in a contiguous table or a run-on list, selecting the whole block in one go is fine and much faster. You are marking where the evidence is, not counting.

                        The same value repeated does not need marking twice. A paper that uses 1:1000 for six antibodies has one dilution, not six.

                        If you stop before you have them all, tick 'There are more of these than I marked'. That box matters more than it looks: without it, someone who stopped at five of eight cannot be told apart from someone who found everything, and the three they skipped would count against the extractor as mistakes it did not make.

                        What counts

                        Judge only what is written. Not what the authors almost certainly did, and not what is standard practice in the field.

                        Judge the text shown. The page shows the Methods section, and sometimes figure legends or the full text where no Methods heading was found. A detail in the text shown counts, legend included. A detail found only in a legend or supplementary file that is not shown is Not stated.

                        Unclear is a real answer. It is set aside rather than counted either way, so use it when you are genuinely stuck rather than guessing.

                        The shaded sentences

                        The faint tint marks the western blot section: sentences that name the blot, the gel, the membrane or its detection. It is the same for every question on a paper. Blot section jumps between its blocks. Other assays are left untinted, but an item can still be stated elsewhere, for example in a figure legend.

                        The warmer marks are the sentences most likely to hold the answer to the open question, and Prev and Next step between them.

                        Both are search aids and nothing more. Fixed keyword rules pick them; they are never told the answer, they sometimes shade irrelevant sentences and sometimes miss the right one. Read around them, and judge only the text.

                        Every question, in one place

                        Antibody validation statedA statement that an antibody's specificity was checked experimentally: knockdown or knockout, a blocking peptide, overexpression, or a positive and a negative control sample stated as checking the antibody's specificity. Routine assay controls, normal serum or no-primary substitutions, isotype controls, a single band at the expected size, a vendor's claim, or a citation alone does not count. Counts for an antibody used in any assay, not only the blot.
                        Mark: Mark the statement. In antibody specificity was confirmed in PAX6 knockdown cells mark the whole phrase. Nothing to mark in a single band was detected at the expected molecular weight, the primary antibody was replaced with normal rabbit serum as a negative control, or validated by the manufacturer.
                        Signal within dynamic range statedA statement that the blot's signal was checked to lie within the linear or dynamic range, or to be unsaturated: a loading or dilution series, an unsaturated-exposure check, or the imager's saturation indicator. 'Optimal exposure', or an exposure time alone, does not count. Western blot only.
                        Mark: Mark the statement. In exposures were chosen so that no band was saturated mark the whole phrase. Nothing to mark in the optimal exposure was used.
                        Fraction purity shownA statement that subcellular fractions run on the blot were checked for purity or cross-contamination with compartment markers. A fraction marker used only as a loading control, or a fractionation kit named alone, does not count. Western blot only.
                        Mark: Mark the statement. In the purity of nuclear and cytosolic fractions was confirmed with lamin B1 and GAPDH mark the whole phrase. Nothing to mark in nuclear proteins were extracted with the NE-PER kit.
                        Sample layout across gelsHow groups were placed on gels: all on the same gel, randomized, or balanced across gels. Western blot only.
                        Mark: Mark the statement. In samples from each group were distributed across gels mark the whole phrase.
                        Membrane pore sizeThe blot membrane's pore size in micrometers. A syringe-filter, Transwell or insert pore size does not count, and neither do other numbers near the membrane (a thickness, a voltage). Western blot only.
                        Mark: Mark the number and its unit. In a 0.45 um PVDF membrane mark 0.45 um.
                        Secondary antibody host or targetThe host species of a secondary antibody, or the species it recognizes, such as goat anti-rabbit or anti-mouse IgG. The conjugate or vendor alone does not count, and neither does a primary antibody's host. Counts for an antibody used in any assay, not only the blot.
                        Mark: Mark the host and target together where they sit together. Mark each distinct combination once. In HRP-conjugated goat anti-rabbit IgG mark goat anti-rabbit.
                        Antibody lot numberA lot or batch number for an antibody. A catalog number does not count. Counts for an antibody used in any assay, not only the blot.
                        Mark: Mark every distinct lot number. In (Abcam, ab1234, lot GR147853-1) mark GR147853-1.
                        Blocking agent sourceThe supplier, product or grade of the blocking agent: 'BSA (Sigma, fraction V)', 'non-fat dry milk (Bio-Rad)', or a named commercial blocking buffer such as Intercept Blocking Buffer. The agent or its percentage alone does not count. Western blot only.
                        Mark: Mark the supplier, product or grade words. In blocked in 5% BSA (Sigma-Aldrich) mark Sigma-Aldrich. Nothing to mark in blocked in 5% non-fat milk.
                        Between-blot normalizationHow samples run on different gels or membranes were made comparable: a common reference sample on every blot, or an inter-blot calibrator. Putting all groups on one gel is sample layout, not this item. Western blot only.
                        Mark: Mark the statement. In a pooled reference sample was loaded on every gel to normalize between blots mark the whole phrase.
                        Transfer buffer compositionThe transfer buffer's recipe (for example Tris-glycine with 20% methanol), or the product used. A recipe with at least one component and its concentration counts, and so does a product identified by brand name, vendor or catalog number. 'Transfer buffer' or 'Tris-glycine buffer' alone does not count. Western blot only.
                        Mark: Mark the recipe, or the product with what identifies it. In in Tris-glycine buffer containing 20% methanol mark the whole phrase.
