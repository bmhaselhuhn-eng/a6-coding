What counts as a western blot. Western blot means proteins separated on a gel, transferred to a membrane and detected with an antibody. An OxyBlot counts. A dot blot or slot blot, of RNA or of protein, does not; neither does a capillary system such as Wes or Jess, which has no membrane, or a membrane probed only with streptavidin or a lectin, which uses no antibody. Items marked 'Western blot only' do not count the same thing described for another assay in the paper: qPCR, immunofluorescence, IHC, flow cytometry, ELISA, mass spectrometry, or a gel that was stained rather than blotted. Judge the text the page shows, including any figure legend printed in it; a detail found only in a legend or supplement that is not shown is not stated. A method given only by citation ('as previously described [12]') is not stated: mark only what the text itself says. The Shortcut citation item is the exception: it records the citation phrase itself. Some PDFs drop the micro sign or print it as m ('50 g', '25 mg' for micrograms); rule on the intended unit.

Every question carries its own What to mark rule with a worked example. Follow it rather than your instinct: consistency between reviewers matters more here than any single choice. Full rules under Guide.

The purpose of our study was to observe whether Rg1 therapy improves cognitive dysfunc- tion and neuronal lesion in T2DM mice, and if so, what the mech- anisms are. Our data demonstrated that Rg1 treatment ameliorated cognitive dysfunction and neuronal lesions, decreased ROS accu- mulation and Ab generation, and inhibited PLCeCNeNFAT1 signaling in T2DM mice, suggesting that Rg1 may be a promising agent for the therapy of DACD. T2DM is a global health issue that can lead to several compli- cations, one of which is DACD, which affects approximately 60-70 percent of people and manifests as a decline in learning and memory abilities, deficiency of concentration and analytical capa- bility and so on [31,32]. Currently, DACD has received lots of attention in the therapy of diabetic complications because of its negative effects, which include neuropathy and cognitive dysfunction [33,34]. Similar to earlier reports [35], our study also proved that T2DM mice exhibited significant cognitive dysfunction in behavior tests. The results of HE and Nissl staining demonstrated severe neuronic injuries in the cortical and hippocampal zones of T2DM mice. Furthermore, synaptic impairment was considered an important representation in the development of DACD [36]. We further confirmed that the expressions of both SYN and PSD95 were decreased in the cortical and hippocampal zones in T2DM mice, but were considerably restored by Rg1 and metformin medication. And Rg1 and metformin treatment significantly ameliorated motor ac- tivity and spatial learning and memory dysfunction and attenuated neuronic lesions induced by T2DM. These findings show that Rg1 therapy protects against neuronal damage and cognitive dysfunc- tion in T2MD mice. Currently, oxidative stress is thought to play an important role in the process of DACD and AD onset and progression. The over- production of ROS can cause neuronal injury, resulting in Ab overproduction and synaptic dysfunction [37]. NOX2 is a key generator of ROS in cerebral neurons and involved in neurode- generative disorders [38]. It has been reported that NOX2 activity is linked to aging-associated neuronic injury and cognitive dysfunc- tion [39]. Our prior findings have proved that NOX4 activation is an important mechanism in diabetic nephropathy [40]. Our latest research also suggested that Rg1 alleviates neuronic injury and Ab deposition through decreasing NOX2 expression in APP/PS1 double-transgenic mice [41]. However, it is still unknown whether NOX2 involves in DACD. In the current investigation, we discovered that Rg1 remarkably reduced ROS generation in the cortical and hippocampal zones in T2DM mice. Furthermore, Rg1 therapy lowered the protein and mRNA expressions of p47phox p22phox, and NOX2, which is the key enzyme in neuronal ROS generation [27]. Our findings revealed the importance of NOX2-mediated ROS during the progression of DACD, which may relevant to the pro- tective effect of Rg1 against DACD. However, how ROS accumulation causes Ab overproduction drew our attention. Numerous reports indicated that calcium (Ca2þ) dysregulation was related to the pathogenesis of various neurological sicknesses such as AD [42,43]. We speculated that Ca2þ overload may participate in DACD. Our data proved that the levels of Ca2þ in the brain were abnormally elevated in T2DM mice and ameliorated by Rg1 treatment, suggesting the involvement of calcium overload in DACD. ROS accumulation has been reported to play an important function in regulating Ca2þ influx and Ca2þ storage by activating PLC [44]. PLC is a widespread intracellular enzyme that catalyzes PIP2 conversion to DAG and IP3 [45]. IP3R located on the ER membrane can be activated by IP3 causing Ca2þ to be released rapidly from the endoplasmic reticulum to the cyto- plasm [45], while DAG can activate TRPC6 inducing extracellular Ca2þ influx [46]. TRPC6 is a Ca2þ-permeable non-selective cation channel with six-fold higher permeability to Ca2þ than to Naþ, it is suspected to be implicated in the pathophysiology of DM [47,48]. Our previous study also indicated that upregulation of TRPC6 in- volves in renal fibrosis in diabetic nephropathy [49]. In our findings, the levels of DAG, IP3 and Ca2þ markedly increased in T2DM mice brain tissues and Rg1 treatment could decrease the concentration of DAG, IP3 and Ca2þ. Further study indicated that Rg1 therapy considerably lowered the enhanced expressions of p-PLC and TRPC6 in T2DM mice brain tissues. These findings implied that Rg1 may ameliorate neuronal Ca2þ overload by inhibiting PLC-TRPC6 signaling in T2DM mice. Recent research reported that CN, a Ca2þ-dependent protein phosphatase, is participated in many signaling pathways in cells. The nuclear factor of activated T cells (NFAT) can be activated by CN, which regulates gene transcription and leads to Ab generation [50,51]. For instance, it has been re- ported that NFAT1 was significantly activated in Tg2576 mouse brains, and Ca2þ overload could activate CN-NFAT1 signaling and promote BACE1 gene expression by directly binding to the BACE1 promoter region [52,53]. In addition, NFAT plays a role in adipo- genesis and glucose/insulin homeostasis, while hyperglycemia significantly up-regulated Ca2þ/CN/NFAT4 axis [54,55]. These re- sults implied that calcium homeostasis and the related CN/NFAT signaling pathway play a key role in diabetes-related complica- tions. In the current study, we found that Rg1 therapy ameliorated calcium overload and inhibited the expressions of CN and NFAT1 and nuclear translocation of NFAT1 in T2DM mice. Based on pre- vious reports and our existing results, we deduced that Rg1 exerts therapeutic effects through the CN/NFAT1 signaling pathway. Accumulating evidence has suggested diabetes as an indepen- dent risk factor for AD. Existing epidemiological evidence also suggests that T2DM elevates the relative risk of dementia and morbidity of AD [31]. Although the pathogenesis of DACD is yet unclear, it's worth noting that T2DM and AD share a lot of simi- larities [56]. For instance, growing evidence suggests that T2DM patients have learning and memory impairments, as well as increased Ab accumulation and neurofibrillary tangles due to Tau protein hyperphosphorylation, both of which are pathological features of AD [57]. APP is a transmembrane protein that could be cleaved into Ab through proteases such as b-secretase and g-sec- retase [58]. BACE1 is the functional protein of b-secretase, and NCSTN is one of the core subunits of g-secretase, which plays a vital role in regulating Ab generation and may be an important target in AD therapeutics [59]. BACE1 hydrolyzes APP to produce CTF-b, which is then endoproteolyzed by g-secretase to produce Ab [60]. The levels of Ab1-42 and p-Tau/Tau were to be considerably higher in T2DM mice in this investigation. This analysis comprised six species of Bufonidae (Bufotes viridis, Bufotes boulengeri, Bufotes luristanicus, Bufotes surdus, Epidalea calamita, and Rhinella marina) and three species from other anuran families Discoglossus galganoi (Alytidae), Xenopus tropicalis (Pipidae), and Pelophylax perezi (Ranidae) (Table 1). Interestingly, positive hybridization signals corresponding to BamHI-800 sequences were only observed in Bufonidae (Supplementary Fig. S4a), with all species showing the same ladder- like pattern: ca. 800 bp, 1600 bp, and 2400 bp, corresponding to monomers, dimers, and trimers. Due to limitations in sample availability, the chromosomal distribution of BamHI-800 repet- itive DNA could only be analysed in four bufonid species: Bufo bufo (Italy), Bufo spinosus (Spain), E. calamita (Spain) and Bufotes viridis (Greece). The karyotypes of these species have 2n = 22, with six pairs of large and five pairs of small metacentric or submetacentric chromosomes. In situ hybridization using a species-specific BamHI-800 probe produced positive signals located at species-specific positions (Fig. 4). Bufo bufo samples display intense positive hybridization signals on the centromere and short arm of the small- est pair of the karyotype (chromosome 11). Other faint signals are also observed on pericentromeric regions of all large chromosomal pairs, on the long arm of the 6th chromosomal pair, next to the secondary constriction corresponding to the NOR, and on the long arm of chromosome pair 7, close to the centromere (Fig. 4a,b). The hybridization pattern in Bufo spinosus is similar to Bufo bufo, except for the absence of a positive signal on chromosome 7 (Fig. 4c,d). The signals are more evident after three rounds of immunological amplification (Figs. 4e,f and 5a). The presence and the intensity of the signal on the long arm of chromosome pairs 2 and 5 (close to the telomere and to the centromere, respectively) showed variability among samples. This variability suggests the existence of polymorphisms in the amount of BamHI-800 repetitive DNA in these chromosomal locations. Analysis of more samples from different geographic locations should be performed to check this hypothesis. The positive signals in E. calamita sit on the short arm of two of the largest chromosome pairs (chromosomes 1 and 3). In both pairs, BamHI-800 is located close to the centromere, with the signal of pair 1 being more intense than that of pair 3 (Figs. 4g and 5b). In Bufotes viridis BamHI-800 is located on the 3rd chromosomal pair, in pericentromeric position at both sides of the centromere, with the most intense signal on the short arm. In addition, there is a positive signal in the chromosomal pair that carries the NOR (pair 6), located on the long arm and close to the centromere (Figs. 4h and 5c).                       Guide

                      You do not type anything

                      Select the words in the Methods text, then press Use highlighted text. The exact words you selected are what gets recorded.

                      Typing is what this design removes. Given a sentence like the one below, one person types RIPA, another types the whole parenthesis, a third types the clause. All three read the paper correctly and disagree in the data. Selecting removes that entirely.

                      The cell total protein was isolated using RIPA (Applygen Technologies Inc., Beijing, China). Protease inhibitor (CWBIO, Shanghai, China) was added into the RIPA at a ratio of 1:100.

                      For lysis buffer there, mark just RIPA, the first time it appears.

                      How much to select

                      The shortest phrase that states the detail. Not the whole sentence when three words will do, and not the vendor, city or catalog number unless the question asks for them.

                      Every question shows a worked example under What to mark. Follow it rather than your instinct.

                      When a paper gives several

                      Many papers list a table of antibodies with eight RRIDs, eight catalog numbers and several vendors. Mark them all. Press Use highlighted text once per value and they stack up in a numbered list. Press Done, next when the list is complete.

                      If they sit in a contiguous table or a run-on list, selecting the whole block in one go is fine and much faster. You are marking where the evidence is, not counting.

                      The same value repeated does not need marking twice. A paper that uses 1:1000 for six antibodies has one dilution, not six.

                      If you stop before you have them all, tick 'There are more of these than I marked'. That box matters more than it looks: without it, someone who stopped at five of eight cannot be told apart from someone who found everything, and the three they skipped would count against the extractor as mistakes it did not make.

                      What counts

                      Judge only what is written. Not what the authors almost certainly did, and not what is standard practice in the field.

                      Judge the text shown. The page shows the Methods section, and sometimes figure legends or the full text where no Methods heading was found. A detail in the text shown counts, legend included. A detail found only in a legend or supplementary file that is not shown is Not stated.

                      Unclear is a real answer. It is set aside rather than counted either way, so use it when you are genuinely stuck rather than guessing.

                      The shaded sentences

                      The faint tint marks the western blot section: sentences that name the blot, the gel, the membrane or its detection. It is the same for every question on a paper. Blot section jumps between its blocks. Other assays are left untinted, but an item can still be stated elsewhere, for example in a figure legend.

                      The warmer marks are the sentences most likely to hold the answer to the open question, and Prev and Next step between them.

                      Both are search aids and nothing more. Fixed keyword rules pick them; they are never told the answer, they sometimes shade irrelevant sentences and sometimes miss the right one. Read around them, and judge only the text.
