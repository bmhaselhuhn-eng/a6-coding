What counts as a western blot. Western blot means proteins separated on a gel, transferred to a membrane and detected with an antibody. An OxyBlot counts. A dot blot or slot blot, of RNA or of protein, does not; neither does a capillary system such as Wes or Jess, which has no membrane, or a membrane probed only with streptavidin or a lectin, which uses no antibody. Items marked 'Western blot only' do not count the same thing described for another assay in the paper: qPCR, immunofluorescence, IHC, flow cytometry, ELISA, mass spectrometry, or a gel that was stained rather than blotted. Judge the text the page shows, including any figure legend printed in it; a detail found only in a legend or supplement that is not shown is not stated. A method given only by citation ('as previously described [12]') is not stated: mark only what the text itself says. The Shortcut citation item is the exception: it records the citation phrase itself. Some PDFs drop the micro sign or print it as m ('50 g', '25 mg' for micrograms); rule on the intended unit.

Every question carries its own What to mark rule with a worked example. Follow it rather than your instinct: consistency between reviewers matters more here than any single choice. Full rules under Guide.

Discussion Fungal keratitis is an ocular infection disease. Its pathogenic mechanism mainly emphasizes chronic inflammation and immune dysregulation [17]. Our results demonstrate that galectin-3, an essential immunological marker, participated in the immune responses and increased obviously in differ- ent stages of fungal keratitis. Moreover, neutrophils were intensely activated in the course of fungal keratitis, and the positive correlation and colocation of galectin-3 and neutro- phils suggested that galectin-3 may play an immune regula- tory role by recruiting neutrophils in the early and late stages of fungal keratitis. The role of galectin-3 in fungal keratitis has not been studied previously. Our finding of a significant increase of galectin-3 in tear samples of patients with fungal keratitis suggests that galectin-3 might be an indicator of the initia- tion of immune reactions in human fungal keratitis. The expression of galectin-3 in the HCEC cell model infected by zymosan, the specific antigen of fungi, suggests that human corneal epithelial cells secrete galectin-3 after recog- nizing the fungi invasion. It has been reported that the recruitment of neutrophils to the site of infection is critical for the clearance of the invading fungi, although other fac- tors governing the host response are responsible for sensing the fungal invasion [18]. Galectin-3 has been reported as a markedly upregulated factor in mice fungi pulmonary infec- tion. It possibly regulates fungal clearance mainly by pro- moting neutrophil activation migration in the lung fungal infection mice model [19, 20]. This research agrees with our data on the correlation between neutrophils and galectin-3 in FK.                       The faint tint marks the western blot section: sentences that name the blot, the gel, the membrane or its detection. It is the same for every question on a paper. Blot section jumps between its blocks. Other assays are left untinted, but an item can still be stated elsewhere, for example in a figure legend.

                      The warmer marks are the sentences most likely to hold the answer to the open question, and Prev and Next step between them.

                      Both are search aids and nothing more. Fixed keyword rules pick them; they are never told the answer, they sometimes shade irrelevant sentences and sometimes miss the right one. Read around them, and judge only the text.

                      Every question, in one place

                      Antibody cloneA clone name or number for a monoclonal antibody. A catalog number, or a vendor's epitope code for a polyclonal (Santa Cruz C-20), does not count. Counts for an antibody used in any assay, not only the blot.
                      Mark: Mark every distinct clone id. In anti-beta-actin (clone AC-15, Sigma) mark AC-15.
                      Molecular weight markerA molecular weight marker or protein ladder stated as run on the blot's gel, named or not, including one named in the SDS-PAGE paragraph for the gel that was blotted. A band size or predicted weight alone, or troubleshooting advice, does not count. Western blot only.
                      Mark: Mark the marker name, or the words stating a marker was run. In alongside PageRuler Prestained Protein Ladder mark PageRuler Prestained Protein Ladder. Nothing to mark in a band at 53 kDa or in an antibody table listing predicted sizes.
                      Quantification procedureHow the blot's bands were quantified beyond naming software: background subtraction, the measurement (integrated density, area under the peak), or how bands were selected. 'Densitometry was performed' does not count, and neither does normalization alone, which is its own item. Western blot only.
                      Mark: Mark the words that describe the procedure. In integrated density was measured after rolling-ball background subtraction mark the whole phrase.
                      Secondary antibody vendorThe company that supplied a secondary antibody, including the maker of a kit that supplies it. 'All antibodies were from X' counts. Counts for an antibody used in any assay, not only the blot.
                      Mark: Mark every distinct company name. Leave out the city and country. A table can be marked as one block. In HRP-conjugated goat anti-rabbit IgG (Jackson ImmunoResearch) mark Jackson ImmunoResearch.
                      Membrane pore sizeThe blot membrane's pore size in micrometers. A syringe-filter, Transwell or insert pore size does not count, and neither do other numbers near the membrane (a thickness, a voltage). Western blot only.
                      Mark: Mark the number and its unit. In a 0.45 um PVDF membrane mark 0.45 um.
                      Transfer buffer compositionThe transfer buffer's recipe (for example Tris-glycine with 20% methanol), or the product used. A recipe with at least one component and its concentration counts, and so does a product identified by brand name, vendor or catalog number. 'Transfer buffer' or 'Tris-glycine buffer' alone does not count. Western blot only.
                      Mark: Mark the recipe, or the product with what identifies it. In in Tris-glycine buffer containing 20% methanol mark the whole phrase.
                      Antibody lot numberA lot or batch number for an antibody. A catalog number does not count. Counts for an antibody used in any assay, not only the blot.
                      Mark: Mark every distinct lot number. In (Abcam, ab1234, lot GR147853-1) mark GR147853-1.
                      Splicing disclosedA statement that lanes were spliced, rearranged or taken from different parts of a blot or from different blots, or that none were. Silence does not count. Western blot only.
                      Mark: Mark the statement. In lanes were rearranged from the same membrane, indicated by dividing lines mark the whole phrase.
