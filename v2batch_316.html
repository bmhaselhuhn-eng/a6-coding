What counts as a western blot. Western blot means proteins separated on a gel, transferred to a membrane and detected with an antibody. An OxyBlot counts. A dot blot or slot blot, of RNA or of protein, does not; neither does a capillary system such as Wes or Jess, which has no membrane, or a membrane probed only with streptavidin or a lectin, which uses no antibody. Items marked 'Western blot only' do not count the same thing described for another assay in the paper: qPCR, immunofluorescence, IHC, flow cytometry, ELISA, mass spectrometry, or a gel that was stained rather than blotted. Judge the text the page shows, including any figure legend printed in it; a detail found only in a legend or supplement that is not shown is not stated. A method given only by citation ('as previously described [12]') is not stated: mark only what the text itself says. The Shortcut citation item is the exception: it records the citation phrase itself. Some PDFs drop the micro sign or print it as m ('50 g', '25 mg' for micrograms); rule on the intended unit.

Every question carries its own What to mark rule with a worked example. Follow it rather than your instinct: consistency between reviewers matters more here than any single choice. Full rules under Guide.

Lysis buffer composition
The lysis buffer's recipe, or the product used. A recipe with at least one component of the base buffer and its concentration counts, and so does a product identified by brand name, vendor or catalog number. A generic name alone, such as 'RIPA buffer', does not count, because RIPA recipes differ, and neither do protease or phosphatase inhibitors added to it, which are their own item. Western blot only.

CALI Targeting GLUT1. Because di-iodinated BODIPY (I2BODIPY) was found to possess an excellent protein inactivation ability in the screening of photosensitizers for CALI, we went on to investigate protein inactivation targeting GLUT1, a glucose transporter known to be overexpressed in many cancer cells. CONCLUSIONS We hereby demonstrated intracellular CALI for molecule- targeted PDT. A photosensitizer characterized by high singlet oxygen production and cell membrane permeability is essential for the molecule-targeted PDT. We synthesized several CAII ligand-conjugated photosensitizers and evaluated their protein inactivation ability using CAII as a protein inactivation model in vitro. I2BODIPY was found to exhibit excellent protein inactivation ability under the irradiation of light at 540 nm wavelength. Moreover, we synthesized a glucose-conjugated I2BODIPY, compound 8, which was observed to target GLUT1 for cancer cell-specific molecule-targeted PDT. We found compound 8 to afford excellent GLUT1-specific protein inactivation, without affecting other housekeeping proteins, such as α-tubulin and PCNA; indeed, treatment with compound 8 resulted in significant light-induced cytotoxicity mediated by modulation of the EGFR/MAPK signaling pathway. These results demonstrate the potential of a novel molecule-targeted PDT and postulate spatiotemporally controllable drug discovery through the light-induced selective inactivation of tumor- specific proteins. 4.                       Guide

                      You do not type anything

                      Select the words in the Methods text, then press Use highlighted text. The exact words you selected are what gets recorded.

                      Typing is what this design removes. Given a sentence like the one below, one person types RIPA, another types the whole parenthesis, a third types the clause. All three read the paper correctly and disagree in the data. Selecting removes that entirely.

                      The cell total protein was isolated using RIPA (Applygen Technologies Inc., Beijing, China). Protease inhibitor (CWBIO, Shanghai, China) was added into the RIPA at a ratio of 1:100.

                      For lysis buffer there, mark just RIPA, the first time it appears.

                      How much to select

                      The shortest phrase that states the detail. Not the whole sentence when three words will do, and not the vendor, city or catalog number unless the question asks for them.

                      Every question shows a worked example under What to mark. Follow it rather than your instinct.

                      When a paper gives several

                      Many papers list a table of antibodies with eight RRIDs, eight catalog numbers and several vendors. Mark them all. Press Use highlighted text once per value and they stack up in a numbered list. Press Done, next when the list is complete.

                      If they sit in a contiguous table or a run-on list, selecting the whole block in one go is fine and much faster. You are marking where the evidence is, not counting.

                      The same value repeated does not need marking twice. A paper that uses 1:1000 for six antibodies has one dilution, not six.

                      If you stop before you have them all, tick 'There are more of these than I marked'. That box matters more than it looks: without it, someone who stopped at five of eight cannot be told apart from someone who found everything, and the three they skipped would count against the extractor as mistakes it did not make.

                      What counts

                      Judge only what is written. Not what the authors almost certainly did, and not what is standard practice in the field.

                      Judge the text shown. The page shows the Methods section, and sometimes figure legends or the full text where no Methods heading was found. A detail in the text shown counts, legend included. A detail found only in a legend or supplementary file that is not shown is Not stated.

                      Unclear is a real answer. It is set aside rather than counted either way, so use it when you are genuinely stuck rather than guessing.

                      The shaded sentences

                      The faint tint marks the western blot section: sentences that name the blot, the gel, the membrane or its detection. It is the same for every question on a paper. Blot section jumps between its blocks. Other assays are left untinted, but an item can still be stated elsewhere, for example in a figure legend.

                      The warmer marks are the sentences most likely to hold the answer to the open question, and Prev and Next step between them.

                      Both are search aids and nothing more. Fixed keyword rules pick them; they are never told the answer, they sometimes shade irrelevant sentences and sometimes miss the right one. Read around them, and judge only the text.
