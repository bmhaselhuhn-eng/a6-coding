What counts as a western blot. Western blot means proteins separated on a gel, transferred to a membrane and detected with an antibody. An OxyBlot counts. A dot blot or slot blot, of RNA or of protein, does not; neither does a capillary system such as Wes or Jess, which has no membrane, or a membrane probed only with streptavidin or a lectin, which uses no antibody. Items marked 'Western blot only' do not count the same thing described for another assay in the paper: qPCR, immunofluorescence, IHC, flow cytometry, ELISA, mass spectrometry, or a gel that was stained rather than blotted. Judge the text the page shows, including any figure legend printed in it; a detail found only in a legend or supplement that is not shown is not stated. A method given only by citation ('as previously described [12]') is not stated: mark only what the text itself says. The Shortcut citation item is the exception: it records the citation phrase itself. Some PDFs drop the micro sign or print it as m ('50 g', '25 mg' for micrograms); rule on the intended unit.

                    2.1 Animals and models Female Sprague-Dawley (SD) rats (about 250 g) were purchased from the Experimental Animal Center of Army Medical University (Chongqing, China). All rats were housed in cages with a 12 h light/dark cycle and a designated temperature of 25 ± 2 °C. All animals were allowed free access to enough food and water. The phase of the estrous cycle in female rats was determined by vaginal smears, and non-estrous female rats were used for the study to avoid the interference of the estrous cycle. All experimental procedures and protocols were approved by the Institutional Animal Care and Use Committee of Army Medical University (protocol number: AMUWEC20212093) and conformed to the National Institutes of Health Guide for the Care and Use of Laboratory Animals. All rats were randomly divided into three groups: the control group, TNBS-3-day group, and TNBS-7-day group. According to a previous report (Ustinova et al., 2007), a single dose of 2, 4, 6-trinitrobenzene sulfonic acid (TNBS) was applied to induce colitis in rats by Frontiers in Physiology frontiersin.org 02 Dong et al. 10.3389/fphys.2022.920044 intrarectal administration. After being anesthetized under isoflurane, the TNBS-3-day and TNBS-7-day rats were administered an intrarectal enema (8 cm from the anus) of 100 mg/kg TNBS dissolved in 50% ethanol. Control rats were administered an intrarectal saline enema. Morphological assessment of colitis was determined by the disease activity index (DAI, 0-12 score) as previously described (Nooh and Nour-Eldien, 2016; Da Silva et al., 2018) and clinically characterized by the presence of loose stools or diarrhea (0-4 sore), rectal bleeding (0-4 sore), and significant weight loss (0-4 sore). 2.2 Tissue sections and staining After 3 or 7 days of intrarectal administration, the rats (n = 4 for each group) were anesthetized with excess sodium pentobarbital. Subsequently, segments of colon and bladder tissues were removed and fixed with 4% paraformaldehyde. After being dehydrated and embedded in paraffin, the colon and bladder tissues were cut into 5-μm-thick tissue sections. The hematoxylin and eosin staining was performed on the colon and bladder tissue sections to observe the pathological change. The thickness of the muscular wall of the distal colon was measured, and the visceral damage score was performed by providing scores ranging from 1 to 5, according to inflammation, infiltration, and vascular density of the tissue. (1, no inflammation; 2, very low inflammation; 3, low level of infiltration; 4, high level of infiltration and vascular density; 5, transmural infiltrations and high vascular density).                       The shortest phrase that states the detail. Not the whole sentence when three words will do, and not the vendor, city or catalog number unless the question asks for them.

                      When a paper gives several

                      Many papers list a table of antibodies with eight RRIDs, eight catalog numbers and several vendors. Mark them all. Press Use highlighted text once per value and they stack up in a numbered list. Press Done, next when the list is complete.

                      If they sit in a contiguous table or a run-on list, selecting the whole block in one go is fine and much faster. You are marking where the evidence is, not counting.

                      The same value repeated does not need marking twice. A paper that uses 1:1000 for six antibodies has one dilution, not six.

                      If you stop before you have them all, tick 'There are more of these than I marked'. That box matters more than it looks: without it, someone who stopped at five of eight cannot be told apart from someone who found everything, and the three they skipped would count against the extractor as mistakes it did not make.

                      Judge only what is written. Not what the authors almost certainly did, and not what is standard practice in the field.

                      Judge the text shown. The page shows the Methods section, and sometimes figure legends or the full text where no Methods heading was found. A detail in the text shown counts, legend included. A detail found only in a legend or supplementary file that is not shown is Not stated.
