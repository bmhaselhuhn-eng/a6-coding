What counts as a western blot. Western blot means proteins separated on a gel, transferred to a membrane and detected with an antibody. An OxyBlot counts. A dot blot or slot blot, of RNA or of protein, does not; neither does a capillary system such as Wes or Jess, which has no membrane, or a membrane probed only with streptavidin or a lectin, which uses no antibody. Items marked 'Western blot only' do not count the same thing described for another assay in the paper: qPCR, immunofluorescence, IHC, flow cytometry, ELISA, mass spectrometry, or a gel that was stained rather than blotted. Judge the text the page shows, including any figure legend printed in it; a detail found only in a legend or supplement that is not shown is not stated. A method given only by citation ('as previously described [12]') is not stated: mark only what the text itself says. The Shortcut citation item is the exception: it records the citation phrase itself. Some PDFs drop the micro sign or print it as m ('50 g', '25 mg' for micrograms); rule on the intended unit.

Discussion Although AD pathogenesis has specific immune and inflammatory mechanisms, the general characteristics include excessive infiltration of inflammatory cells, such as lympho- cytes, macrophages, and granulated mast cells into skin lesions, eosinophilia in peripheral blood, and high levels of serum IgE [20]. Importantly, the central pathophysiology in AD is the generation of allergen-specific IgE, most of which is Th2-mediated. We previously reported that GJExCR treatment improved AD skin symptoms by enhancing skin barrier factors in DfE-induced NC/Nga mice [18]. In the present study, we demonstrated the effect of GJExCR treatment on an OVA-induced allergic reaction. Release of proinflammatory IgE from plasma cells, mast cells, and eosinophils is important in OVA-induced allergy, as levels of serum IgE increase after antigen-specific IgE stimulation via intranasal challenge with OVA [21]. Here, we show that GJExCR treatment reduced the Th2-mediated immune response in OVA-induced allergy, including dampening of IL-4, IL-5, IL-10, IL-13, TNF-α, INF-γ, chemokine, and IgE expression, in both serum and spleens. GJExCR treatment dampened the allergy response via suppression of proinflammatory factor expression, which ultimately inhibited the Th2 response. Consistent with this finding, a recent study attenuated an allergic response by modulation of Th1/Th2 cytokine and IgE levels, which inhibited the proinflammatory response [22]. Interestingly, Th2-dominant immune response results from an imbalance between Th2 and Th1 actions, which plays a crucial role in the development of AD [23]. We also investigated CD4+ T helper cells in OVA-induced allergy response. CD4+ T cells play a pivotal role in infection, inflammation, and autoimmunity through cytokine release, including IL-4, IL-13, and IL-17, which induces a cascade of reactions that act against allergens [24,25]. GJExCR treatment reduced CD4+ and CD8+ T cell populations in lymph nodes and spleen. OVA-specific CD4+ and CD8+ T cell proliferation appeared first in lymph nodes and later in the spleen, suggesting a possible migration of activated cells from the site of induction into systemic circulation. CD4+ T cells support the generation of CD8+ T cells in this system. Interestingly, we observed a significant correlation between the Cells 2023, 12, 941 11 of 13 percentages of proliferating CD4+ and CD8+ T cells for each animal [26]. Previous studies found that vaccines able to activate both CD4+ and CD8+ T cell responses result in better immunity in patients [27]. Naïve CD4+ T cells differentiate into a number of specialized Th cell subsets. In addition, repression of STAT1 decreased the effects of downstream STAT1-dependent inflammatory mediators, including secondary effects on inflammatory cells, including Th cell cytokine and chemokine production, Ag-specific Ig levels, and mucous cell metaplasia in OVA induced allergy model [28]. Reports show that IL-4 induces the differentiation of naïve CD4+ T cells to Th2 cells via binding to the IL-4 receptor. CD4+ T cells can be subdivided into Th1, Th2, Th17, and Treg subsets on the basis of their pattern of cytokine production [29]. In our study, STAT1 increased within HaCaT cells after TNF-α/IFN-γ stimulation. However, this STAT1 increase was suppressed after GJExCR treatment, as was expression of other Th2 type chemokines and cytokines. IL-4 and IL-13, Th2 cytokines, decrease the expression of epidermal differentiation complex genes filaggrin, loricrin, and involucrin in differentiated primary human keratinocytes [30]. Interestingly, we previously reported that GJExCR treatment strengthened skin barriers in DfE induced NC/Nga mice [18]. Furthermore, the GJExCR compound geniposide had the same effect as GJExCR treatment, but crocin had no effect on the immune response. Long-term use of topical corticosteroids, particularly those of high potency, cause common side effects. Although many studies have examined the use of herbal medicines for the treatment of individual or multiple health conditions [31], insufficient evidence currently exist to support the use of herbs for the treatment of AD [32]. Our results support GJExCR as a potential anti-allergic agent for the clinical management of AD. 5. Conclusions This investigation demonstrated the anti-allergic potential of GJExCR against OVA- induced AD. GJExCR exerted its effect by inhibition of allergen-induced hypersensitivity via inhibition of p38 MAPK/STAT1 activation, resulting in the regulation of Th2 response and decreased release of CD4+ T cells, CD8+ T cells, and IgE. Results and Discussion In order to evaluate the role of cAMP in modulating MRP4 expression in pancreatic cell lines, MRP4 mRNA transcripts were assessed by real-time PCR. PANC-1 cells, a widely used epithe- lial ductal pancreatic carcinoma model, were exposed to forskolin (adenylyl cyclase direct acti- vator), IBMX (PDE inhibitor) and db-cAMP (cAMP permeable analog). Following cell exposure, all the agents assessed led to an approximate 2-fold increase in MRP4 mRNA levels as compared with untreated cells. These results indicate that cAMP increasing agents up-regu- late MRP4. It is worth noting that cycloheximide treatment abolished db-cAMP-stimulated in- crease in MRP4 mRNA, suggesting that de novo synthesis is required for cAMP-mediated MRP4 transcription (Fig. 1A). In accordance, when MRP4 protein levels were analyzed by western blot, we observed a significant increase in MRP4 expression in cells exposed to forsko- lin, db-cAMP and IBMX (Fig. 1B).                       Many papers list a table of antibodies with eight RRIDs, eight catalog numbers and several vendors. Mark them all. Press Use highlighted text once per value and they stack up in a numbered list. Press Done, next when the list is complete.

                      If they sit in a contiguous table or a run-on list, selecting the whole block in one go is fine and much faster. You are marking where the evidence is, not counting.

                      The same value repeated does not need marking twice. A paper that uses 1:1000 for six antibodies has one dilution, not six.

                      If you stop before you have them all, tick 'There are more of these than I marked'. That box matters more than it looks: without it, someone who stopped at five of eight cannot be told apart from someone who found everything, and the three they skipped would count against the extractor as mistakes it did not make.

                      What counts

                      Judge only what is written. Not what the authors almost certainly did, and not what is standard practice in the field.

                      Judge the text shown. The page shows the Methods section, and sometimes figure legends or the full text where no Methods heading was found. A detail in the text shown counts, legend included. A detail found only in a legend or supplementary file that is not shown is Not stated.

                      Unclear is a real answer. It is set aside rather than counted either way, so use it when you are genuinely stuck rather than guessing.

                      The shaded sentences

                      The faint tint marks the western blot section: sentences that name the blot, the gel, the membrane or its detection. It is the same for every question on a paper. Blot section jumps between its blocks. Other assays are left untinted, but an item can still be stated elsewhere, for example in a figure legend.

                      The warmer marks are the sentences most likely to hold the answer to the open question, and Prev and Next step between them.

                      Both are search aids and nothing more. Fixed keyword rules pick them; they are never told the answer, they sometimes shade irrelevant sentences and sometimes miss the right one. Read around them, and judge only the text.

                      Every question, in one place

                      Transfer conditionsThe voltage or current, the time, or the temperature of the transfer. Any one counts. The transfer method alone ('wet transfer') does not. Western blot only.
                      Mark: Mark the conditions. In transferred at 100 V for 1 h at 4 °C mark the whole phrase.
                      Uncropped blots providedA statement that full, uncropped or original images of the blots are provided, in a main figure, a supplement or a public repository, including membranes cut into strips shown as run. 'Available on request', or a data-availability statement that does not mention blot images, does not count. Western blot only.
                      Mark: Mark the statement. In the original blots can be obtained in the supplementary material mark the whole phrase. Nothing to mark in raw data are available from the corresponding author on request.
                      Blocking agent sourceThe supplier, product or grade of the blocking agent: 'BSA (Sigma, fraction V)', 'non-fat dry milk (Bio-Rad)', or a named commercial blocking buffer such as Intercept Blocking Buffer. The agent or its percentage alone does not count. Western blot only.
                      Mark: Mark the supplier, product or grade words. In blocked in 5% BSA (Sigma-Aldrich) mark Sigma-Aldrich. Nothing to mark in blocked in 5% non-fat milk.
                      Membrane cutting disclosedA statement that the membrane was cut into strips or pieces before antibody incubation, so that targets were probed on separate pieces. Cropping the image for the figure does not count. Western blot only.
                      Mark: Mark the statement. In membranes were cut at the 50 kDa marker and probed separately mark the whole phrase. Nothing to mark in blots were cropped for clarity.
                      Reducing or denaturing conditionsA statement of reducing or denaturing conditions for the blot's samples: Laemmli buffer; sample or loading buffer with heating; a stated denaturing temperature; a reducing agent (DTT, 2-ME); the condition named ('denatured'); or an explicit non-reducing statement. A reducing agent in a lysis or fractionation buffer feeding the blot counts. SDS-PAGE alone, an SDS running buffer, or heating in elution buffer does not. Western blot only.
                      Mark: Mark the phrase or the reagent that states it. In boiled in Laemmli buffer containing DTT mark DTT, or boiled in Laemmli buffer if no reagent is named. Nothing to mark in separated by SDS-PAGE, SDS running buffer, or heated in elution buffer.
                      Antibody cloneA clone name or number for a monoclonal antibody. A catalog number, or a vendor's epitope code for a polyclonal (Santa Cruz C-20), does not count. Counts for an antibody used in any assay, not only the blot.
                      Mark: Mark every distinct clone id. In anti-beta-actin (clone AC-15, Sigma) mark AC-15.
                      Secondary antibody catalog numberA vendor catalog or product number for a secondary antibody. A primary antibody's catalog number, or a lot number, does not count. Counts for an antibody used in any assay, not only the blot.
                      Mark: Mark every distinct catalog number. A table can be marked as one block. In HRP-conjugated goat anti-rabbit IgG (Jackson ImmunoResearch, 111-035-144) mark 111-035-144.
                      Membrane activationA pre-transfer membrane step: PVDF pre-wetted in methanol, or the membrane equilibrated in transfer buffer. Equilibrating the gel does not count. Western blot only.
                      Mark: Mark the step. In PVDF membranes were activated in methanol for 1 min mark the whole phrase.
                      Exposure timeAn exposure or acquisition time for the blot's signal, on film or on an imager. 'Auto-exposure', 'different exposure times', 'exposed to film' with no time, or a substrate incubation time ('incubated with ECL for 1 min') does not count, and neither does an antibody or cytokine array, which is not a blot. Western blot only.
                      Mark: Mark the time and its unit. In bands were imaged with a 30 s exposure on a ChemiDoc mark 30 s. Nothing to mark in incubated with ECL for 1 min or cells were exposed to 80 uM drug for 30 min.
