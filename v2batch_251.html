What counts as a western blot. Western blot means proteins separated on a gel, transferred to a membrane and detected with an antibody. An OxyBlot counts. A dot blot or slot blot, of RNA or of protein, does not; neither does a capillary system such as Wes or Jess, which has no membrane, or a membrane probed only with streptavidin or a lectin, which uses no antibody. Items marked 'Western blot only' do not count the same thing described for another assay in the paper: qPCR, immunofluorescence, IHC, flow cytometry, ELISA, mass spectrometry, or a gel that was stained rather than blotted. Judge the text the page shows, including any figure legend printed in it; a detail found only in a legend or supplement that is not shown is not stated. A method given only by citation ('as previously described [12]') is not stated: mark only what the text itself says. The Shortcut citation item is the exception: it records the citation phrase itself. Some PDFs drop the micro sign or print it as m ('50 g', '25 mg' for micrograms); rule on the intended unit.

Involvement of nAChR. Recently, we discovered that stimulation of nAChR containing subunits α7, α9, and α10 efficiently inhibits the BzATP-triggered release of IL-1β by primary human monocytes and by LPS-primed U937 cells [18, 20]. This pathway is specific for ATP-dependent inflam- masome activation as it inhibits the ion channel function of receptor P2X7. This prompted us to test if CCL3 also inhibits the ATP-independent IL-1β release induced by the pore- forming bacterial toxin nigericin (50 μM). In this experimen- tal setting, we added apyrase (0.5 U/ml), an enzyme that effi- ciently cleaves endogenous ATP that might be released by LPS-primed and nigericin-treated U937 cells. Stimulation of LPS-primed U937 cells with nigericin/apyrase for 30 min induced IL-1β secretion as described before (Figure 4(a)) [20]. Of note, nigericin-induced secretion of IL-1β was not reduced by CCL3 (Figure 4(a)). This result is in line with our assumption that CCL3 triggers a mechanism that is spe- cific for ATP-dependent inflammasome activation. In the next set of experiments, we tested the rather pro- vocative hypothesis that nAChR are involved in CCL3 signal- ing using a panel of nAChR antagonists that do not induce the release of IL-1β when applied alone [18, 20] (Figure 4(b)). The inhibitory effect of CCL3 on BzATP- induced release of IL-1β by U937 cells was indeed sensitive to mecamylamine (100 μM; p = 0 001, n = 5 versus n = 9), a general nAChR antagonist. Similarly, α-bungarotoxin (1 μM; p = 0 001, n = 5 versus n = 9) and strychnine (10 μM; p = 0 001, n = 5 versus n = 9), reagents that preferentially antagonize nAChR containing subunits α7 and α9, were effi- cient (Figure 4(b)). To differentiate between those subunits, we made use of α-conotoxins ArIB [V11L,V16D] (500 nM), specific for nAChR containing an α7 subunit, and RgIA4 (200 nM), an antagonist of α9α10 nAChR [16-19]. Both α- conotoxins antagonized the effect of CCL3 (p = 0 003, n = 5 versus n = 9), suggesting that signal transduction involves nAChR subunits α7 and α9 (Figure 4(b)). These nAChR sub- units previously have been shown to be expressed by U937 cells and by human monocytes [20, 21]. These results are in line with the idea that chemokine sig- naling triggers a recently described mechanism involving activation of metabotropic functions of noncanonical nAChR containing subunits α7 and α9 that inhibit P2X7 sig- naling and, consequently, BzATP-induced release of IL-1β [18, 20]. Of note, the inhibitory effect of acetylcholine (10 μM) was not impaired by silencing of CCR expression (Figure 3(c)), suggesting that nAChR are acting downstream of CCR. 3.5. Release of Soluble Factors. We wanted to solve the ques- tion of how CCR activation links to cholinergic signaling. As at least some of the effective nicotinic antagonists such as α-bungarotoxin and the α-conotoxin RgIA4 probably do not enter the cytoplasm of the target cell due to their size and hydrophilicity, we assume that relevant nAChR are localized in the plasma membrane. Hence, we postulated the involvement of a soluble small nicotinic agonist that is released to the cell culture medium in response to CCL3. To test this hypothesis, LPS-primed U937 cells were stimulated with CCL3, conditioned cell culture medium was harvested 30 min later, and a low molecular weight frac- tion of this medium devoid of CCL3 was produced by ultra- filtration (Figure 5(a)). As a control, conditioned medium was produced in the absence of CCL3, but CCL3 was added to the cell-free supernatant shortly before ultrafiltration. In line with our hypothesis, the ultrafiltrate of conditioned medium significantly inhibited the BzATP-stimulated release of IL-1β by LPS-primed U937 cells, whereas the ultrafiltrate of the control conditioned medium was ineffec- tive (p = 0 016; n = 4 versus n = 5) (Figure 5(b)). In the current study, the expression of wildtype and mutant forms of eIF2α was exogenous. Due to polyploidy, methods for homologous recombination and gene replacement are not yet available for E. histolytica. As such, in all of the transgenic cell lines, endogenous eIF2α was present. Despite this limitation, we observed phenotypes in the transgenic cell lines. As expected, cells over-expressing wildtype or phosphomimetic variants of eIF2α exhibited increased or decreased translation, respectively. Thus, these exogenous proteins were able to apparently compete with endogenous eIF2α in a predicted fashion. Surprisingly, in cells expressing the non-phosphorylatable variant, EheIF2α-S59A, polyri- bosome abundance and protein biosynthesis were decreased. This phenotype was unexpected because non-phosphorylated eIF2α is generally considered a driver of translation.                       Guide

                      You do not type anything

                      Select the words in the Methods text, then press Use highlighted text. The exact words you selected are what gets recorded.

                      Typing is what this design removes. Given a sentence like the one below, one person types RIPA, another types the whole parenthesis, a third types the clause. All three read the paper correctly and disagree in the data. Selecting removes that entirely.

                      The cell total protein was isolated using RIPA (Applygen Technologies Inc., Beijing, China). Protease inhibitor (CWBIO, Shanghai, China) was added into the RIPA at a ratio of 1:100.

                      For lysis buffer there, mark just RIPA, the first time it appears.

                      How much to select

                      The shortest phrase that states the detail. Not the whole sentence when three words will do, and not the vendor, city or catalog number unless the question asks for them.

                      Every question shows a worked example under What to mark. Follow it rather than your instinct.

                      When a paper gives several

                      Many papers list a table of antibodies with eight RRIDs, eight catalog numbers and several vendors. Mark them all. Press Use highlighted text once per value and they stack up in a numbered list. Press Done, next when the list is complete.

                      If they sit in a contiguous table or a run-on list, selecting the whole block in one go is fine and much faster. You are marking where the evidence is, not counting.

                      The same value repeated does not need marking twice. A paper that uses 1:1000 for six antibodies has one dilution, not six.

                      If you stop before you have them all, tick 'There are more of these than I marked'. That box matters more than it looks: without it, someone who stopped at five of eight cannot be told apart from someone who found everything, and the three they skipped would count against the extractor as mistakes it did not make.

                      What counts

                      Judge only what is written. Not what the authors almost certainly did, and not what is standard practice in the field.

                      Judge the text shown. The page shows the Methods section, and sometimes figure legends or the full text where no Methods heading was found. A detail in the text shown counts, legend included. A detail found only in a legend or supplementary file that is not shown is Not stated.

                      Unclear is a real answer. It is set aside rather than counted either way, so use it when you are genuinely stuck rather than guessing.

                      The shaded sentences

                      The faint tint marks the western blot section: sentences that name the blot, the gel, the membrane or its detection. It is the same for every question on a paper. Blot section jumps between its blocks. Other assays are left untinted, but an item can still be stated elsewhere, for example in a figure legend.

                      The warmer marks are the sentences most likely to hold the answer to the open question, and Prev and Next step between them.

                      Both are search aids and nothing more. Fixed keyword rules pick them; they are never told the answer, they sometimes shade irrelevant sentences and sometimes miss the right one. Read around them, and judge only the text.

                      Getting through a batch

                      The Methods text stays pinned on the left while questions scroll on the right. Answers collapse to one line and the next question opens automatically. Click any collapsed line to change it.

                      Your work is saved in this browser as you go, so closing the tab loses nothing. Submit is at the top of the page and turns on once every question is answered and your initials are filled in.
