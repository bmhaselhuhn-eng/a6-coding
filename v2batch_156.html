What counts as a western blot. Western blot means proteins separated on a gel, transferred to a membrane and detected with an antibody. An OxyBlot counts. A dot blot or slot blot, of RNA or of protein, does not; neither does a capillary system such as Wes or Jess, which has no membrane, or a membrane probed only with streptavidin or a lectin, which uses no antibody. Items marked 'Western blot only' do not count the same thing described for another assay in the paper: qPCR, immunofluorescence, IHC, flow cytometry, ELISA, mass spectrometry, or a gel that was stained rather than blotted. Judge the text the page shows, including any figure legend printed in it; a detail found only in a legend or supplement that is not shown is not stated. A method given only by citation ('as previously described [12]') is not stated: mark only what the text itself says. The Shortcut citation item is the exception: it records the citation phrase itself. Some PDFs drop the micro sign or print it as m ('50 g', '25 mg' for micrograms); rule on the intended unit.

Every question carries its own What to mark rule with a worked example. Follow it rather than your instinct: consistency between reviewers matters more here than any single choice. Full rules under Guide.

DMOG, an inhibitor of prolyl hydroxylase, has been shown to stabilize or increase HIF-1α protein and enhance the expression of downstream target genes (Jaakkola et al., 2001; Milkiewicz et al., 2004). It was reported that the in- hibition of endogenous HIF-1 inactivation by DMOG in- duces angiogenesis in ischemic skeletal muscles of mice (Milkiewicz et al., 2004). Accumulation of HIF-1α induced by DMOG seems to protect tissue against myocardial isch- emia injury within 3 hr (Zhao et al., 2010) and then to in- duce angiogenesis in ischemic skeletal muscles of mice after several days (Milkiewicz et al., 2004) or in human critical limb ischemia over 2 weeks (Ho et al., 2006). In the previous study, we observed that increases in expression of HIF-1α protein were induced in the DRGs after the injec- tion of DMOG into the hindlimb muscles (Gao & Li, 2013). However, BP response evoked by static muscle contraction in occluded rats was not significantly amplified by DMOG. In contrast, data of the present study indicate that blocking HIF-1α in DRGs of occluded rats by BAY87 can attenuate the amplified BP response to muscle contraction. Published studies have demonstrated that femoral artery occlusion induces greater expression of muscle metabo- lite sensitive receptors, that is, transient receptor potential vanilloid type 1 (TRPV1), purinergic P2X3, and acid-sens- ing ion channels (ASICs), etc. (Li & Xing, 2012; Xing et al., 2015). Also, response amplitude of the DRG neurons with the activation of those receptors is enhanced in oc- cluded rats (Xing et al., 2008, 2009, 2012, 2013). These alterations in expression and response of those metabolic receptors lead to augments in muscle afferent nerve-me- diated SNA and BP responses. In specific, it has been re- ported (Ristoiu et al., 2011) that hypoxia, one of the most dominant conditions during tissue ischemia, increased the sensitization of TRPV1 in the DRG neurons. The sensiti- zation of TRPV1, which is relied on its phosphorylation of the serine residues and the translocation of protein ki- nase C (PKC)ε, was inhibited by the administration of a HIF-1α inhibitor 2-methoxyestradiol (Ristoiu et al., 2011). Meanwhile, the level of TRPV1 phosphorylation and the PKCε translocation were positively linked to the expres- sion of HIF-1α. These results suggest that HIF-1α is in- volved in the process of TRPV1 sensitization via the PKCε pathway. In our present study, BAY-87 significantly atten- uated HIF-1α expression in the DRG at 3 hr following its administration (Figure 1b,c), whereas the blood pressure response to static muscle contraction in occluded rats was suppressed at 3 and 3.5 hr after BAY87 injection (Figure 3c). It, therefore, indicates that the inhibition of HIF-1α and subsequent alternation of the exercise pressor reflex was likely due to the reduced potentization of TRPV1 in the muscle sensory nerves. In addition, a number of prior reports suggest that nerve growth factor (NGF) can increase expression of TRPV1, P2X, and ASIC in the DRG neurons (Anand et al., 2006; Mamet et al., 2002, 2003; Ramer et al., 2001). Interestingly, the pre- vious work has shown that increasing HIF-1α or inhibiting HIF-1α prolyl hydroxylases can attenuate NGF deprivation induced-effects on neurons, suggesting that HIF-1α plays a regulatory role in the activity of NGF (Farinelli & Greene, 1996; Lomb et al., 2007; Xie et al., 2005). Thus, it is specu- lated that NGF may contribute to the effects of HIF-1α on the augmented exercise pressor reflex by the alteration of muscle metabolic receptors in the DRG neurons after femoral artery occlusion. In conclusion, our data show that femoral artery occlusion augments expression of HIF-1α protein in the DRGs, and en- hances BP response induced by static muscle contraction. BAY87 injected into the arterial blood supply of the hind- limb muscles decreases the protein levels of HIF-1α in the DRGs and attenuates the amplified reflex pressor response in occluded rats during muscle contraction. Overall, it is impli- cated that the inhibition of HIF-1α alleviates the exaggeration of the exercise pressor reflex in rats under ischemic circum- stances of the hindlimbs as seen in PAD. With consideration of the time efficacy of BAY-87 on HIF-1α inhibition (3 hr following its administration) and the subsequent beneficial effect on attenuating the exaggerated exercise pressor reflex in the simulated PAD model, it shed light upon the develop- ment of the future therapeutic strategy for the clinical PAD patients.

Conclusions Data from the current study highlight that both slaughter age and postmortem time may affect the properties of the Longissimus lumborum muscles of Sahraoui dromedaries and provide insights into protein changes during the conversion of muscle into meat. Post- mortem time in adult and young dromedaries resulted in different meat tenderization rates. In particular, an early and intense postmortem proteolysis involves meat from young dromedaries, as evidenced by the degradation of key proteins of the tenderization process, such as desmin and α-actinin. These findings have also been supported by the more intense and faster troponin T degradation found in younger animals, which confirmed a different kinetic in the disruption and degradation of myofibrils during the postmortem time, highlighting a different proteolytic potential between dromedaries of different ages.                       Exposure timeAn exposure or acquisition time for the blot's signal, on film or on an imager. 'Auto-exposure', 'different exposure times', 'exposed to film' with no time, or a substrate incubation time ('incubated with ECL for 1 min') does not count, and neither does an antibody or cytokine array, which is not a blot. Western blot only.
                      Mark: Mark the time and its unit. In bands were imaged with a 30 s exposure on a ChemiDoc mark 30 s. Nothing to mark in incubated with ECL for 1 min or cells were exposed to 80 uM drug for 30 min.
                      Substrate incubation timeHow long the membrane was incubated in the detection substrate before imaging. An exposure or imaging time does not count. Western blot only.
                      Mark: Mark the time. In incubated with ECL for 1 min mark 1 min. Nothing to mark in an exposure time.
                      Antibody isotypeThe immunoglobulin class or subclass stated for an antibody itself, primary or secondary (IgG, IgG1, IgG2a, IgM, IgY), as in 'rabbit polyclonal IgG' or 'mouse monoclonal IgG2a'. An isotype that names what a secondary recognizes ('goat anti-rabbit IgG'), an isotype control, a normal or control IgG, or a serum antibody response does not count. Host species and clonality belong to Primary antibody host or type. Counts for an antibody used in any assay, not only the blot.
                      Mark: Mark each distinct isotype once. In mouse monoclonal IgG2a anti-GFAP mark IgG2a; in rabbit polyclonal IgG anti-Kif5 mark IgG. Nothing to mark in HRP-conjugated goat anti-rabbit IgG.
                      Normalization statedHow the blot's band intensities were normalized, with the reference named: to a loading control, to total protein, or phospho to total ('normalized to beta-actin' is enough; the arithmetic need not be spelled out). Expressing values relative to a control group alone does not count, and neither does naming a loading control without saying the bands were normalized to it, a statistics statement that names no assay, or a pooled reference sample used only to compare blots (between-blot normalization). Western blot only.
                      Mark: Mark the phrase that states the calculation. In band intensities were normalized to beta-actin and expressed relative to control mark normalized to beta-actin. Nothing to mark in quantitated as percent of DMSO, GAPDH was used as a loading control, or gene expression was normalized to GAPDH (qPCR).
                      Antibody validation statedA statement that an antibody's specificity was checked experimentally: knockdown or knockout, a blocking peptide, overexpression, or a positive and a negative control sample stated as checking the antibody's specificity. Routine assay controls, normal serum or no-primary substitutions, isotype controls, a single band at the expected size, a vendor's claim, or a citation alone does not count. Counts for an antibody used in any assay, not only the blot.
                      Mark: Mark the statement. In antibody specificity was confirmed in PAX6 knockdown cells mark the whole phrase. Nothing to mark in a single band was detected at the expected molecular weight, the primary antibody was replaced with normal rabbit serum as a negative control, or validated by the manufacturer.
                      Total protein stainA total-protein stain used to normalize or check the blot: Ponceau S or REVERT on the membrane, stain-free imaging used for normalization, or Coomassie stated as the blot's loading or transfer check, including a parallel gel stained and stated as the blot's loading check. A stained gel with no stated link to a blot, or a stain-free gel named only as a product, does not count. Western blot only.
                      Mark: Mark the stain name only. In confirmed by Ponceau S staining mark Ponceau S. Nothing to mark in run on a 4-15% TGX Stain-Free gel with no normalization stated, in any stain on a purity, expression, preparative, mass-spec or antibody-QC gel, or in a stain on a gel that was never blotted and is not stated as the blot's loading check.
                      Between-blot normalizationHow samples run on different gels or membranes were made comparable: a common reference sample on every blot, or an inter-blot calibrator. Putting all groups on one gel is sample layout, not this item. Western blot only.
                      Mark: Mark the statement. In a pooled reference sample was loaded on every gel to normalize between blots mark the whole phrase.
                      Blinded quantification statedA statement that the blot's band quantification or image analysis was done blind to group or treatment. Blinding of another assay, of animal allocation, or of the study as a whole without naming the blot analysis does not count. Western blot only.
                      Mark: Mark the statement. In densitometry was performed by an investigator blinded to treatment group mark the whole phrase. Nothing to mark in histology was scored by a blinded pathologist.
                      Gel and running buffer systemThe gel chemistry or the running buffer, either one (Tris-glycine, Bis-Tris, MOPS or MES, Tris-acetate, Tricine), or a named precast gel product. 'SDS-PAGE' alone, or a gel percentage alone, does not count. Western blot only.
                      Mark: Mark the words naming the system or product. In NuPAGE 4-12% Bis-Tris gels in MES running buffer mark the whole phrase.
                      Molecular weight markerA molecular weight marker or protein ladder stated as run on the blot's gel, named or not, including one named in the SDS-PAGE paragraph for the gel that was blotted. A band size or predicted weight alone, or troubleshooting advice, does not count. Western blot only.
                      Mark: Mark the marker name, or the words stating a marker was run. In alongside PageRuler Prestained Protein Ladder mark PageRuler Prestained Protein Ladder. Nothing to mark in a band at 53 kDa or in an antibody table listing predicted sizes.
