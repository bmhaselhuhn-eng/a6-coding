What counts as a western blot. Western blot means proteins separated on a gel, transferred to a membrane and detected with an antibody. An OxyBlot counts. A dot blot or slot blot, of RNA or of protein, does not; neither does a capillary system such as Wes or Jess, which has no membrane, or a membrane probed only with streptavidin or a lectin, which uses no antibody. Items marked 'Western blot only' do not count the same thing described for another assay in the paper: qPCR, immunofluorescence, IHC, flow cytometry, ELISA, mass spectrometry, or a gel that was stained rather than blotted. Judge the text the page shows, including any figure legend printed in it; a detail found only in a legend or supplement that is not shown is not stated. A method given only by citation ('as previously described [12]') is not stated: mark only what the text itself says. The Shortcut citation item is the exception: it records the citation phrase itself. Some PDFs drop the micro sign or print it as m ('50 g', '25 mg' for micrograms); rule on the intended unit.

Every question carries its own What to mark rule with a worked example. Follow it rather than your instinct: consistency between reviewers matters more here than any single choice. Full rules under Guide.

A majority of gastric cancer patients show chromosomal instability (CIN) resulting in aneu- ploidy.4,12,13 It has been suggested that tumour cells acquire aberrant chromosome numbers and other chromosomal defects as a result of deregulation of mechanisms responsible for maintaining the chro- mosomal number stability, such as spindle assem- bly checkpoint and chromosome segregation.14,15 However, mutations in mitotic genes are rare, due to the fact that severe defects of these genes would trigger cell death by cell-surveillance early in the development.14-17 Studies revealed that subtle changes in mitotic segregation genes, controlling chromatids separation or regulating the progress of mitosis, could be prime candidates for inducing the slow process of accumulation of genetic chang- es, leading to CIN.15,18-20 The novel hypothesis is further supported by the fact that this process is slow, and explains the late onset of sporadic epi- thelial cancers21,22, as well as heterogeneous muta- tion load observed in different sections of tumours from individual patients. The multidomain protein kinase BUB1B (BUB1- related kinase, known as MAD3 in yeast) plays a central role in the process of spindle assembly checkpoint (SAC), which prevents defects in the segregation of sister chromatids by delaying their separation until all chromatids have achieved cor- rect attachments to the mitotic spindle.23,24 BUB1B is part of the mitotic checkpoint complex (MCC), which together with BUB3, MAD2 and CDC20 inhibits the anaphase-promoting complex/cyclo- some (APC/C), delaying the onset of anaphase and ensuring proper chromosome segregation.25 The protein BUB1B has also been localized to the kinetochores and is important for stabilizing the kinetochore-microtubule interactions and chro- mosome alignment.26 A dual specificity protein kinase TTK (alias MPS1) is crucial for the spindle assembly checkpoint, for chromosome biorienta- tion on the mitotic spindle and for ensuring ac- curate chromosome segregation.27,28 Inhibitor and chemical genetics studies showed that TTK activity facilitates the conformational activation of MAD2 from open to closed form (C-MAD2) capable of CDC20 binding and inhibition, thus delaying the onset of anaphase.29 TTK is probably implicated in the recruitment of the MAD1-C-MAD2 complex to kinetochores and during mitosis its activity is con- tinuously required to recruit O-Mad2 to the Mad1- C-Mad2 core.30 Furthermore, TTK is required for CENP-E recruitment, whose activity is essential for metaphase chromosome alignment.30 In the present study we examined polymor- phisms rs151658 (C>G) in TTK gene, rs1031963 (C>T) and rs1801376 (A>G) in BUB1B gene in the population of Slovenian patients with an advanced gastric cancer and their impact on gastric cancer risk. Discussion USP18 is a cysteine protease with specific ISG15 cleavage (deconjugating) activity [33]. Originally cloned from leuke- mia fusion protein AML1-ETO-expressing mice [34], USP18 is an ISG whose expression level is inducible by type I IFN and is degraded by proteolysis through the SCFSkp2 ubiquitin ligase [35]. The roles of the USP18 pathway in viral infection differ depending on the virus involved and can be mediated through multiple different routes. Although origi- nally attributed to the effects of ISGylation, subsequently it has been shown that USP18 has ISG15-independent effects. Although Usp18-/- mice exhibited less replication of LCMV and VSV, ISG15- or Ube1L-knockout mice had the same sensitivity to LCMV and VSV infection as wild-type mice, indicating that USP18 may behave in an ISG15- independent manner [36]. Specifically, murine USP18 can bind to human IFNAR2 and block type I IFN signaling by competitively interfering with Jak1 binding to the receptor [28]. Thus, at least some of its effects may be mediated through its effects on IFN signaling and not through ISG15 directly. And USP18 also has the ability to affect cellular pathways (and expression of surface proteins), as demon- strated by its ability to (1) regulate the expression of the EGF receptor in carcinoma cells [37, 38], (2) inhibit tumor necrosis factor- (TNF-) related apoptosis-inducing ligand- (TRAIL-) induced apoptosis [39], and (3) employ USP20 to promote deubiquitination of the mitochondrial adaptor pro- tein STING [40]. Overexpression of either USP18 WT or protease-inactive USP18 C64S had no effect in HCV model systems that require only RNA replication (HCV replicon-containing cells). Neither the wild type nor the protease-inactive variant of USP18 altered HCV RNA in two distinct replicon systems. This elevated CD81 expression might be responsible for the increased HCV infectivity and subsequent viral production in Huh7.5 cells. The fact that USP18 expression augments mark- edly increased CD81 expression and HCV infectivity pro- vided some mechanistic insight into the effect and may illustrate a novel means by which HCV can subvert the host innate immune response to its benefit.                       Guide

                      You do not type anything

                      Select the words in the Methods text, then press Use highlighted text. The exact words you selected are what gets recorded.

                      Typing is what this design removes. Given a sentence like the one below, one person types RIPA, another types the whole parenthesis, a third types the clause. All three read the paper correctly and disagree in the data. Selecting removes that entirely.

                      The cell total protein was isolated using RIPA (Applygen Technologies Inc., Beijing, China). Protease inhibitor (CWBIO, Shanghai, China) was added into the RIPA at a ratio of 1:100.

                      For lysis buffer there, mark just RIPA, the first time it appears.

                      How much to select

                      The shortest phrase that states the detail. Not the whole sentence when three words will do, and not the vendor, city or catalog number unless the question asks for them.

                      Every question shows a worked example under What to mark. Follow it rather than your instinct.

                      When a paper gives several

                      Many papers list a table of antibodies with eight RRIDs, eight catalog numbers and several vendors. Mark them all. Press Use highlighted text once per value and they stack up in a numbered list. Press Done, next when the list is complete.

                      If they sit in a contiguous table or a run-on list, selecting the whole block in one go is fine and much faster. You are marking where the evidence is, not counting.

                      The same value repeated does not need marking twice. A paper that uses 1:1000 for six antibodies has one dilution, not six.

                      If you stop before you have them all, tick 'There are more of these than I marked'. That box matters more than it looks: without it, someone who stopped at five of eight cannot be told apart from someone who found everything, and the three they skipped would count against the extractor as mistakes it did not make.

                      What counts

                      Judge only what is written. Not what the authors almost certainly did, and not what is standard practice in the field.

                      Judge the text shown. The page shows the Methods section, and sometimes figure legends or the full text where no Methods heading was found. A detail in the text shown counts, legend included. A detail found only in a legend or supplementary file that is not shown is Not stated.

                      Unclear is a real answer. It is set aside rather than counted either way, so use it when you are genuinely stuck rather than guessing.

                      The shaded sentences

                      The faint tint marks the western blot section: sentences that name the blot, the gel, the membrane or its detection. It is the same for every question on a paper. Blot section jumps between its blocks. Other assays are left untinted, but an item can still be stated elsewhere, for example in a figure legend.

                      The warmer marks are the sentences most likely to hold the answer to the open question, and Prev and Next step between them.

                      Both are search aids and nothing more. Fixed keyword rules pick them; they are never told the answer, they sometimes shade irrelevant sentences and sometimes miss the right one. Read around them, and judge only the text.
