What counts as a western blot. Western blot means proteins separated on a gel, transferred to a membrane and detected with an antibody. An OxyBlot counts. A dot blot or slot blot, of RNA or of protein, does not; neither does a capillary system such as Wes or Jess, which has no membrane, or a membrane probed only with streptavidin or a lectin, which uses no antibody. Items marked 'Western blot only' do not count the same thing described for another assay in the paper: qPCR, immunofluorescence, IHC, flow cytometry, ELISA, mass spectrometry, or a gel that was stained rather than blotted. Judge the text the page shows, including any figure legend printed in it; a detail found only in a legend or supplement that is not shown is not stated. A method given only by citation ('as previously described [12]') is not stated: mark only what the text itself says. The Shortcut citation item is the exception: it records the citation phrase itself. Some PDFs drop the micro sign or print it as m ('50 g', '25 mg' for micrograms); rule on the intended unit.

Every question carries its own What to mark rule with a worked example. Follow it rather than your instinct: consistency between reviewers matters more here than any single choice. Full rules under Guide.

Discussion We have demonstrated that IGFBP-3 protein can bind with H3 protein. Eukaryotic histones proteins are a group of basic proteins that are highly conserved. They are involved in the compaction of DNA forming nucleosomes, which is the first level of the packaging of DNA. Each nucleosome is a hetero-octamer comprising of pairs of H3-H4 dimers associated with pairs of H2A-H2B dimers [36-39] and 147 bp of DNA is wrapped in 1.7 turns around the octamers of histone proteins arranged as a nucleosome [40]. IGFBP-5 is structurally and functionally similar to IGFBP-3 [41] and has also been reported to bind with H3, which was demonstrated by co-immunoprecipitation and prox- imity ligation assays [42]. The binding of IGFBP-5 with H3 can regulate the transcription. Studies using deletion mutations of IGFBP-3 from lamprey demonstrated the presence of transactivation domain in the N-terminal domain [43]. Furthermore, transcriptional regulatory activity has been shown to be associated with the N-terminal domain of human IGFBP-3, IGFBP-4 and IGFBP-5 proteins [42]. Interestingly, transactivation domain was present in the N-terminal domain of IGFBP-5 protein, and the IGFBP-5 peptide containing N-terminal domain exhibited transactivation, however, the full-length IGFBP-5 protein Int. J. Mol. Sci. 2021, 22, 407 6 of 11 failed to exhibit transactivation, suggesting the presence of negative regulatory elements within the IGFBP-5 protein [42]. The full-length IGFBP-5 protein could suppress transcrip- tion, and it was demonstrated that the transcription repression activity was present in the mid-linker domain and the C-terminal domain [42]. N-terminal domain of human IGFBP-3 is also known to possess transactivation domain; however, it is unknown if the full-length protein has transcription negative regulatory elements and, therefore, the effect of the full-length protein on the transactivation of IGFBP-3 remains unknown [42]. Our results indicate that the H3 and IGFBP-3 proteins bind to each other in a concentration- dependent manner and the linear range is in picomoles. Using dot-ligand blot, the linear range for IGFBP-3 binding to IGF-1 was reported to be between 25-1000 nmol, in a study led by Cohen [21]. From our study, it is evident that the binding of IGFBP-3 with H3 does not fall in the range of IGF-1 binding. Our results indicate that the binding affinity of H3 is between 2.33-37.42 pmol, suggesting a stronger binding of IGFBP-3 with H3 compared to IGF-1, as shown in ligand dot blot experiment (Figure 4). The internalization of IGFBP-3 into the nucleus has been reported to be through the direct interaction with importin-β in a non-classical manner [2]. Nuclear IGFBP-3 can function as a transcription factor or a regulator of transcription either directly or indirectly through the interaction with different nuclear hormone receptors [29]. Nuclear IGFBP-3 has also been demonstrated to regulate the process of DNA repair pathways through non-homologous end joining (NHEJ) [44]. The role of IGFBP-3 as a direct regulator of transcription is supported by the study that demonstrated an interaction of IGFBP-3 with RNA polymerase binding subunit 3 (Rpb3), one of the 13 subunits of RNA polymerase [28]. The role of IGFBP-3 as an indirect regulator of transcription through the interac- tion with nuclear hormone receptor was demonstrated by showing the direct interaction with retinoid X receptor-α (RXR-α), which was essential for the mediation of embry- onic growth, development, and differentiation [29]. Apart from RXR-α, IGFBP-3 pro- tein has also been demonstrated to directly interact with other nuclear hormone recep- tors, RAR-α, VDR, PPAR-γ and THR thus supporting its role in indirect regulation of transcription [30,31,45-48]. For the first time, this study identified that H3 protein interacts with nuclear IGFBP-3, which could be suggestive of the role of IGFBP-3 in chromatin remodeling. Chromatin structure is known to undergo compaction and relaxation, which is required to regulate critical cellular processes like DNA replication, transcription, recombination repair, and chromosomal stability. The domains of IGFBP-3 and H3 involved in this interaction are not known and will be investigated in the future. Basic histone proteins, including H3 are known to undergo post-translational modifications like methylation, phosphorylation, acetylation at the N-terminal region, which may play a role in interaction with IGFBP-3. Protein-protein interaction between IGFBP-3 and H3 could prove to be vital in influ- encing structural changes to the genome. Alteration in gene expression is a prime cause for the onset and progression of various diseases, including cancer and other metabolic disorders.                       Guide

                      You do not type anything

                      Select the words in the Methods text, then press Use highlighted text. The exact words you selected are what gets recorded.

                      Typing is what this design removes. Given a sentence like the one below, one person types RIPA, another types the whole parenthesis, a third types the clause. All three read the paper correctly and disagree in the data. Selecting removes that entirely.

                      The cell total protein was isolated using RIPA (Applygen Technologies Inc., Beijing, China). Protease inhibitor (CWBIO, Shanghai, China) was added into the RIPA at a ratio of 1:100.

                      For lysis buffer there, mark just RIPA, the first time it appears.

                      How much to select

                      The shortest phrase that states the detail. Not the whole sentence when three words will do, and not the vendor, city or catalog number unless the question asks for them.

                      Every question shows a worked example under What to mark. Follow it rather than your instinct.

                      When a paper gives several

                      Many papers list a table of antibodies with eight RRIDs, eight catalog numbers and several vendors. Mark them all. Press Use highlighted text once per value and they stack up in a numbered list. Press Done, next when the list is complete.

                      If they sit in a contiguous table or a run-on list, selecting the whole block in one go is fine and much faster. You are marking where the evidence is, not counting.

                      The same value repeated does not need marking twice. A paper that uses 1:1000 for six antibodies has one dilution, not six.

                      If you stop before you have them all, tick 'There are more of these than I marked'. That box matters more than it looks: without it, someone who stopped at five of eight cannot be told apart from someone who found everything, and the three they skipped would count against the extractor as mistakes it did not make.

                      What counts

                      Judge only what is written. Not what the authors almost certainly did, and not what is standard practice in the field.

                      Judge the text shown. The page shows the Methods section, and sometimes figure legends or the full text where no Methods heading was found. A detail in the text shown counts, legend included. A detail found only in a legend or supplementary file that is not shown is Not stated.

                      Unclear is a real answer. It is set aside rather than counted either way, so use it when you are genuinely stuck rather than guessing.

                      The shaded sentences

                      The faint tint marks the western blot section: sentences that name the blot, the gel, the membrane or its detection. It is the same for every question on a paper. Blot section jumps between its blocks. Other assays are left untinted, but an item can still be stated elsewhere, for example in a figure legend.

                      The warmer marks are the sentences most likely to hold the answer to the open question, and Prev and Next step between them.

                      Both are search aids and nothing more. Fixed keyword rules pick them; they are never told the answer, they sometimes shade irrelevant sentences and sometimes miss the right one. Read around them, and judge only the text.

                      Every question, in one place

                      Antibody concentration by massAn antibody working concentration in mass per volume (for example 0.5 ug/mL). A dilution ratio alone does not count. Counts for an antibody used in any assay, not only the blot.
                      Mark: Mark each distinct concentration once. In anti-p53 at 1 ug/mL mark 1 ug/mL.
                      Total protein stainA total-protein stain used to normalize or check the blot: Ponceau S or REVERT on the membrane, stain-free imaging used for normalization, or Coomassie stated as the blot's loading or transfer check, including a parallel gel stained and stated as the blot's loading check. A stained gel with no stated link to a blot, or a stain-free gel named only as a product, does not count. Western blot only.
                      Mark: Mark the stain name only. In confirmed by Ponceau S staining mark Ponceau S. Nothing to mark in run on a 4-15% TGX Stain-Free gel with no normalization stated, in any stain on a purity, expression, preparative, mass-spec or antibody-QC gel, or in a stain on a gel that was never blotted and is not stated as the blot's loading check.
                      Signal within dynamic range statedA statement that the blot's signal was checked to lie within the linear or dynamic range, or to be unsaturated: a loading or dilution series, an unsaturated-exposure check, or the imager's saturation indicator. 'Optimal exposure', or an exposure time alone, does not count. Western blot only.
                      Mark: Mark the statement. In exposures were chosen so that no band was saturated mark the whole phrase. Nothing to mark in the optimal exposure was used.
                      Reducing agent, namedA named reducing agent in the sample or loading buffer for any sample on the blot's gel (a reduced control lane counts), or in a lysis or fractionation buffer whose lysate is run on the blot: DTT, beta-mercaptoethanol (2-ME), TCEP, or a named reducing product such as NuPAGE Sample Reducing Agent. An agent named only as left out ('without DTT') does not count. Western blot only.
                      Mark: Mark each distinct reagent name once. In Laemmli buffer with 5% 2-mercaptoethanol mark 2-mercaptoethanol. Nothing to mark in boiled in Laemmli buffer, with no reagent named, or in DTT used for a mass-spec digest.
                      Antibody immunogenThe antigen used to raise an antibody: a peptide sequence or region, a recombinant fragment, or a full-length protein. The target or a phospho-site given as part of the antibody's name (anti-phospho-ERK Thr202/Tyr204) does not count. Counts for an antibody used in any assay, not only the blot.
                      Mark: Mark the words naming the immunogen. In raised against a synthetic peptide corresponding to residues 1-20 of human p53 mark the whole phrase.
                      Membrane cutting disclosedA statement that the membrane was cut into strips or pieces before antibody incubation, so that targets were probed on separate pieces. Cropping the image for the figure does not count. Western blot only.
                      Mark: Mark the statement. In membranes were cut at the 50 kDa marker and probed separately mark the whole phrase. Nothing to mark in blots were cropped for clarity.
                      Protein loaded per laneA mass of protein loaded on the blot's gel, per lane, well or sample ('Proteins (40 ug) were run on SDS gels' counts). A volume on its own does not count, and neither does a mass used for another assay (an IP or pull-down input, a digest, drug or RNA amounts). Western blot only.
                      Mark: Mark the mass and its unit. In 30 ug of protein was loaded per lane mark 30 ug.
                      Fraction purity shownA statement that subcellular fractions run on the blot were checked for purity or cross-contamination with compartment markers. A fraction marker used only as a loading control, or a fractionation kit named alone, does not count. Western blot only.
                      Mark: Mark the statement. In the purity of nuclear and cytosolic fractions was confirmed with lamin B1 and GAPDH mark the whole phrase. Nothing to mark in nuclear proteins were extracted with the NE-PER kit.
                      Electrophoresis conditionsThe voltage or current, or the run time, for the blot's gel. Either counts. Transfer voltage or time does not. Western blot only.
                      Mark: Mark the conditions. In run at 120 V for 90 min mark the whole phrase.
                      Lysis buffer compositionThe lysis buffer's recipe, or the product used. A recipe with at least one component of the base buffer and its concentration counts, and so does a product identified by brand name, vendor or catalog number. A generic name alone, such as 'RIPA buffer', does not count, because RIPA recipes differ, and neither do protease or phosphatase inhibitors added to it, which are their own item. Western blot only.
                      Mark: Mark the recipe, or the product with what identifies it. In RIPA (50 mM Tris pH 7.4, 150 mM NaCl, 1% NP-40, 0.5% deoxycholate, 0.1% SDS) mark the parenthesis; in RIPA (Applygen Technologies) mark the whole phrase. Nothing to mark in lysed in RIPA buffer.
