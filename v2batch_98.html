What counts as a western blot. Western blot means proteins separated on a gel, transferred to a membrane and detected with an antibody. An OxyBlot counts. A dot blot or slot blot, of RNA or of protein, does not; neither does a capillary system such as Wes or Jess, which has no membrane, or a membrane probed only with streptavidin or a lectin, which uses no antibody. Items marked 'Western blot only' do not count the same thing described for another assay in the paper: qPCR, immunofluorescence, IHC, flow cytometry, ELISA, mass spectrometry, or a gel that was stained rather than blotted. Judge the text the page shows, including any figure legend printed in it; a detail found only in a legend or supplement that is not shown is not stated. A method given only by citation ('as previously described [12]') is not stated: mark only what the text itself says. The Shortcut citation item is the exception: it records the citation phrase itself. Some PDFs drop the micro sign or print it as m ('50 g', '25 mg' for micrograms); rule on the intended unit.

Every question carries its own What to mark rule with a worked example. Follow it rather than your instinct: consistency between reviewers matters more here than any single choice. Full rules under Guide.

Discussion SDS-PAGE, Western blot, and ELISA results confirmed that the AcSBV-rVP1 and AcSBV-rVP2 were successfully expressed in E. coli. Previously, three structural protein genes (VP1, VP2, and VP3) of CSBV (GenBank accession no. HM237361.1) were successfully Viruses 2021, 13, 2439 12 of 15 expressed in the pGEX-6P-1 bacterial expression system using codon optimization, and each recombinant protein was used for the production of polyclonal antiserum [49]. Based on the sequence alignment, the VP1 and VP2 proteins of AcSBV-Kor share 96.4% and 96.6% amino acid identity with those of CSBV. This suggests that the polyclonal antiserum directed against the recombinant proteins (structural proteins) of AcSBV-Kor can cross-react with their structural protein counterparts in CSBV. AcSBV-VP2 comprises the N-terminal end (residue 213) to residue 388 of the coat protein, while AcSBV-VP1 comprises residues from 774 to the C-terminal end (residue 965) of the coat protein. AcSBV-VP2 shares 38.4% nucleotide and 69.8% amino acid identity with AcSBV-VP1 based on the sequence alignment. In this study, rVP1 and rVP2 were evaluated for their antigenicity against their specific antibody and each other. In Western blot analysis, pAb-rV1 showed a strong specificity to the homologous antigen and a weak specificity to the heterologous antigen. pAb-rVP2 reacted strongly with both homologous and heterologous antigens. This suggests that pAb-rVP1 is less sensitive but has a higher specificity than pAb-rVP2. Our results also indicate that rVP2 shares major immune- relevant domains with rVP1 and that both recombinant proteins present authentic viral epitopes. On the other hand, mAb-VP1-1 and mAb-VP2-13 were bound only to their homologous antigens. Both pAbs (pAb-rVP1 and pAb-rVP2) and mAbs (mAb-rVP1 and mAb-rVP2) are able to recognize native virus particles in field samples, providing the required basis for the development of the IC kit. A critical factor of IC assay design is optimal selection of an antibody that shows sensitive and specific detection of a target antigen. Ultimately, the performance of the IC assay relies on the antibody affinity when binding an antigen in a sample. In a typical sandwich format, either a single mAb or two different mAbs are used as a detector antibody and a capture antibody in the IC strips [32]. In the case of low sample loading, the use of the same mAb for detection and capture in the IC assay showed poor detection sensitivity because of competition for the same epitope between the detector and capture mAbs [50]. The use of two different mAbs in the IC assay is an expensive suggestion, limiting its practical usefulness in POCT. Furthermore, the detector mAb binding to an epitope has the possibility of affecting other spatially unrelated epitopes in a way that could modify binding to the capture mAb [51]. Alternatively, mAb has been applied as a detector antibody, while pAb was applied as a capture antibody [32], and vice versa [41]. Utilizing mAb as a detector antibody and pAb as a capture antibody may result in alteration of some of the original epitopes, leaving the rest of the epitopes unaltered and accessible to the polyclonal capture antibodies [32]. Furthermore, pAbs are generally less expensive and faster to produce than mAbs. Hence, the present strip test based on a combination of mAb and pAb provides an acceptable alternative for onsite and cost-effective diagnosis of AcSBV infection. Antibody pairs that can simultaneously bind to the target antigen with high sensitivity and specificity are required for a sandwich assay. In this study, we developed an IC test strip for detecting AcSBV using two pairs of antibodies (pAb-rVP1/mAb-rVP1-1 and pAb-rVP2/mAb-rVP2-13). This design helps to accumulate the target antigen on the pAb-rVP1- or pAb-rVP2-conjugated pad, therefore enhancing the detection of antigen- antibody binding to increase the sensitivity. As shown in Figure 5, the antibody pair of pAb-rVP1/mAb-rVP1-1 showed more sensitivity than pAb-rVP2/mAb-VP2-13 at detecting low concentrations in a sample. Thus, it was hypothesized that the implications of pAbs, which can detect multiple epitopes simultaneously, may replace at least one mAb in the sandwich format of an IC test [32]. Furthermore, this design helps the antigen-pAb complex to react with mAb-rVP1-1 or mAb-rVP2-13 embedded at the test line, therefore enhancing the capturing specificity. In a preliminary test, an IC kit reacted negatively against deformed-wing virus (DWV)-infected adult bee samples. Thus, it is important to ensure that the capture antibody does not recognize other antigens present in a field sample. Currently, there is no commercially available IC test kit for the detection of AcSBV. Viruses 2021, 13, 2439 13 of 15 The specificities and sensitivity of the newly developed test strips described in the present study are also comparable with the real-time PCR method. RT-PCR is a simple, sensitive, and cost-effective method to detect SBV RNA based on the amplification of specific viral sequences [9,23,27]. However, this method is limited to quantifying viral RNA and often produces enormous specificity and sensitivity. Real-time RT-PCR is a better tool for sensitive, accurate, and specific detection and quantification of SBV [25,26,29]. Nevertheless, the two methods are time-consuming and require highly trained personnel to handle specialized laboratory instruments. RT-LAMP detected SBV RNA as soon as 20 min at 65 ◦C, and is suitable for onsite detection in field [24]. Although this method has some advantages, one of the limitations is a high risk of carryover contam- ination which often produces false-positive results in negative samples [52]. Compared with the traditional detection methods, an IC strip method has the advantages of being easy to use, providing an answer rapidly and at a low cost, and not requiring special- ized equipment or technical personnel; thus, such a method is suitable for field testing for antigens. 5. Conclusions This study established a basis for the development of related IC detection kits that are useful for bee farmers and verified their capability for rapidly diagnosis at the time and site of an encounter with environmental samples.                         How much to select

                        The shortest phrase that states the detail. Not the whole sentence when three words will do, and not the vendor, city or catalog number unless the question asks for them.

                        Every question shows a worked example under What to mark. Follow it rather than your instinct.

                        When a paper gives several

                        Many papers list a table of antibodies with eight RRIDs, eight catalog numbers and several vendors. Mark them all. Press Use highlighted text once per value and they stack up in a numbered list. Press Done, next when the list is complete.

                        If they sit in a contiguous table or a run-on list, selecting the whole block in one go is fine and much faster. You are marking where the evidence is, not counting.

                        The same value repeated does not need marking twice. A paper that uses 1:1000 for six antibodies has one dilution, not six.

                        If you stop before you have them all, tick 'There are more of these than I marked'. That box matters more than it looks: without it, someone who stopped at five of eight cannot be told apart from someone who found everything, and the three they skipped would count against the extractor as mistakes it did not make.

                        What counts

                        Judge only what is written. Not what the authors almost certainly did, and not what is standard practice in the field.

                        Judge the text shown. The page shows the Methods section, and sometimes figure legends or the full text where no Methods heading was found. A detail in the text shown counts, legend included. A detail found only in a legend or supplementary file that is not shown is Not stated.

                        Every question, in one place

                        Membrane pore sizeThe blot membrane's pore size in micrometers. A syringe-filter, Transwell or insert pore size does not count, and neither do other numbers near the membrane (a thickness, a voltage). Western blot only.
                        Mark: Mark the number and its unit. In a 0.45 um PVDF membrane mark 0.45 um.
                        Secondary antibody catalog numberA vendor catalog or product number for a secondary antibody. A primary antibody's catalog number, or a lot number, does not count. Counts for an antibody used in any assay, not only the blot.
                        Mark: Mark every distinct catalog number. A table can be marked as one block. In HRP-conjugated goat anti-rabbit IgG (Jackson ImmunoResearch, 111-035-144) mark 111-035-144.
                        Molecular weight markerA molecular weight marker or protein ladder stated as run on the blot's gel, named or not, including one named in the SDS-PAGE paragraph for the gel that was blotted. A band size or predicted weight alone, or troubleshooting advice, does not count. Western blot only.
                        Mark: Mark the marker name, or the words stating a marker was run. In alongside PageRuler Prestained Protein Ladder mark PageRuler Prestained Protein Ladder. Nothing to mark in a band at 53 kDa or in an antibody table listing predicted sizes.
                        Quantified band identifiedA statement of which band or bands of the blot were quantified, by size or form (the 44 kDa band, the cleaved form), or that additional bands were present and documented. Naming the target protein alone, or a band size given only to identify the protein, does not count. Western blot only.
                        Mark: Mark the words that identify the quantified band or the extra bands. In densitometry was carried out on the ~44 kDa band as well as on the smaller ~25 kDa band mark the whole phrase. Nothing to mark in PAR2 protein was quantified.
                        Uncropped blots providedA statement that full, uncropped or original images of the blots are provided, in a main figure, a supplement or a public repository, including membranes cut into strips shown as run. 'Available on request', or a data-availability statement that does not mention blot images, does not count. Western blot only.
                        Mark: Mark the statement. In the original blots can be obtained in the supplementary material mark the whole phrase. Nothing to mark in raw data are available from the corresponding author on request.
                        Quantification procedureHow the blot's bands were quantified beyond naming software: background subtraction, the measurement (integrated density, area under the peak), or how bands were selected. 'Densitometry was performed' does not count, and neither does normalization alone, which is its own item. Western blot only.
                        Mark: Mark the words that describe the procedure. In integrated density was measured after rolling-ball background subtraction mark the whole phrase.
                        Protein loaded per laneA mass of protein loaded on the blot's gel, per lane, well or sample ('Proteins (40 ug) were run on SDS gels' counts). A volume on its own does not count, and neither does a mass used for another assay (an IP or pull-down input, a digest, drug or RNA amounts). Western blot only.
                        Mark: Mark the mass and its unit. In 30 ug of protein was loaded per lane mark 30 ug.
                        Normalization statedHow the blot's band intensities were normalized, with the reference named: to a loading control, to total protein, or phospho to total ('normalized to beta-actin' is enough; the arithmetic need not be spelled out). Expressing values relative to a control group alone does not count, and neither does naming a loading control without saying the bands were normalized to it, a statistics statement that names no assay, or a pooled reference sample used only to compare blots (between-blot normalization). Western blot only.
                        Mark: Mark the phrase that states the calculation. In band intensities were normalized to beta-actin and expressed relative to control mark normalized to beta-actin. Nothing to mark in quantitated as percent of DMSO, GAPDH was used as a loading control, or gene expression was normalized to GAPDH (qPCR).
                        Antibody isotypeThe immunoglobulin class or subclass stated for an antibody itself, primary or secondary (IgG, IgG1, IgG2a, IgM, IgY), as in 'rabbit polyclonal IgG' or 'mouse monoclonal IgG2a'. An isotype that names what a secondary recognizes ('goat anti-rabbit IgG'), an isotype control, a normal or control IgG, or a serum antibody response does not count. Host species and clonality belong to Primary antibody host or type. Counts for an antibody used in any assay, not only the blot.
                        Mark: Mark each distinct isotype once. In mouse monoclonal IgG2a anti-GFAP mark IgG2a; in rabbit polyclonal IgG anti-Kif5 mark IgG. Nothing to mark in HRP-conjugated goat anti-rabbit IgG.
                        Secondary antibody vendorThe company that supplied a secondary antibody, including the maker of a kit that supplies it. 'All antibodies were from X' counts. Counts for an antibody used in any assay, not only the blot.
                        Mark: Mark every distinct company name. Leave out the city and country. A table can be marked as one block. In HRP-conjugated goat anti-rabbit IgG (Jackson ImmunoResearch) mark Jackson ImmunoResearch.
