What counts as a western blot. Western blot means proteins separated on a gel, transferred to a membrane and detected with an antibody. An OxyBlot counts. A dot blot or slot blot, of RNA or of protein, does not; neither does a capillary system such as Wes or Jess, which has no membrane, or a membrane probed only with streptavidin or a lectin, which uses no antibody. Items marked 'Western blot only' do not count the same thing described for another assay in the paper: qPCR, immunofluorescence, IHC, flow cytometry, ELISA, mass spectrometry, or a gel that was stained rather than blotted. Judge the text the page shows, including any figure legend printed in it; a detail found only in a legend or supplement that is not shown is not stated. A method given only by citation ('as previously described [12]') is not stated: mark only what the text itself says. The Shortcut citation item is the exception: it records the citation phrase itself. Some PDFs drop the micro sign or print it as m ('50 g', '25 mg' for micrograms); rule on the intended unit.

Discussion and Perspectives 4.1. Es-KIF2A and Es-PLK1 Are Both Involved in Maintaining the Proliferation of Spermatogenic Cells and Inhibiting the Apoptosis of Spermatogenic Cells Previous studies on KIF2A and PLK1 mainly focused on cell division processes. In this study, we first investigated their roles in spermatogenic cell proliferation and apoptosis in vivo. For the first time, we cloned the Es-Kif2a and Es-Plk1 genes and performed multiple amino acid sequence alignment and evolutionary tree analysis on them (Supplementary Figures S1-S4). These results showed that Es-KIF2A and Es-PLK1 are very conserved evolutionarily, especially when compared to species in the same genus of Crustacea, laying the foundation for the study of crustaceans. Next, Western Blot and EdU/TUNEL staining were performed to detect spermatogenic cell proliferation and apoptosis after Es-Kif2a knockdown or Es-Plk1 inhibition. Our results showed that both Es-Kif2a and Es-Plk1 can affect spermatogenic cell proliferation and apoptosis during E. sinensis spermatogenesis. However, in the future, more methods will be needed to detect the expression of other proliferation/apoptosis-related proteins, and further validation in in vitro cell lines is necessary. Biology 2024, 13, 149 14 of 18 4.2. Effects of Es-KIF2A and Es-PLK1 on the Proliferation and Apoptosis of Spermatogenic Cells Are Realized by Regulating the PI3K/AKT Signaling Pathway Next, our Western Blot results showed that the expression levels of PI3K and AKT proteins decreased significantly after Es-Kif2a knockdown or Es-Plk1 inhibition, indicating that they have a positive regulatory mechanism on PI3K/AKT. AKT activation, on the one hand, promotes the expression of proliferating proteins PCNA, CDK2, and anti-apoptotic protein Bcl-2 and on the other hand, inhibits the expression of pro-apoptotic proteins Bax and Caspase-3 (Figure 9). Thus, our findings demonstrated that KIF2A/PLK1 affects cell proliferation and apoptosis through the PI3K/AKT pathway. Moreover, the immunofluo- rescence results showed that Es-KIF2A and Es-PLK1 co-localized during different stages of E. sinensis spermatogenesis, which demonstrates that KIF2A and PLK1 may coordinate to regulate the PI3K/AKT pathway. These results fill the gap in the regulation of the PI3K/AKT pathway by these two genes. Figure 9. A schematic diagram of KIF2A/PLK1 complex co-regulating PI3K/AKT signaling and proliferation and apoptosis in spermatogenic cells. AKT activation, on the one hand, promotes the expression of proliferating proteins PCNA, CDK2, and anti-apoptotic protein Bcl-2 and on the other hand, inhibits the expression of pro-apoptotic proteins Bax and Caspase-3. However, whether other protein factors are involved in the regulation of PI3K/AKT pathway by KIF2A/PLK1 complex, whether KIF2A/PLK1 complex also regulate other signaling pathways, whether other cell proliferation/apoptosis proteins are also influenced, these questions still requires further study. As we mentioned before, the MRN complex [34] and DVL ligand [35-37] are responsi- ble for KIF2A-PLK1 complex recruitment during mitosis or cilia formation. In this case, are MRN and DVL also involved in the recruitment of the KIF2A/PLK1 complex during the regulation of the PI3K/AKT pathway? Further research is needed to answer this question (Figure 9). Another question requires further research: how does the KIF2A-PLK1 complex regulate the PI3K/AKT pathway? As we mentioned earlier, PLK1 may upregulate the AKT pathway by relieving the inhibitory effect of PTEN on AKT [32]. In addition, we Biology 2024, 13, 149 15 of 18 speculate that some mitotic regulatory factors may be involved in this process (Figure 9). During mitosis, PLK1 interacts and phosphorylates the N-terminus of SPAG5 (also known as astrin) [38-40], which has been reported to upregulate the PI3K/AKT pathway in cancer cells in vitro [41-44] through the centrosome protein CEP55 [45]. In Xenopus, Gwl (MASTL in humans) also interacts with and is phosphorylated by PLK1 during the cell cycle [46]. MASTL-PP2A is a well-known cell cycle checkpoint system, which restrains PI3K/AKT activity during the human cell cycle [47,48]. These speculations all require further research. Furthermore, the following questions require further exploration. Can KIF2A/PLK1 also regulate other signaling pathways related to cell proliferation and apoptosis, like TGF-β, Wnt-β-catenin, MAPK/ERK, JNK, NF-κB, etc.? And do these pathways directly interact with each other through crossover? This is shown in Figure 9. 4.3. Es-PLK1 Is a Downstream Factor of Es-KIF2A At the end of this study, we preliminarily explored the upstream and downstream relationships between Es-KIF2A and Es-PLK1. Western Blot showed that the expression level of the Es-PLK1 protein decreased significantly after Es-Kif2a knockdown, while there was no significant change in Es-KIF2A expression after Es-Plk1 inhibition. Thus, we tentatively conclude that Es-PLK1 is a downstream factor of Es-KIF2A. In the future, more experiments, such as subcellular co-localization, co-immunoprecipitation (co-IP), interaction site/domain analysis, etc., will need to be performed. 4.4. Effects of Es-Kif2a Knockdown or Es-Plk1 Inhibition on Testicular Somatic Cells of E. sinensis Our fluorescence images focused on spermatogenic cells and confirmed the effect of Es-Kif2a knockdown or Es-Plk1 inhibition on spermatogenic cell proliferation and apoptosis. However, in addition to germ cells, E. sinensis testes also contain somatic cells, such as Sertoli cells, Leydig cells, and myoid cells, which constitute the microenvironment or the niche of testes and are essential for regulating normal spermatogenesis. Whether Es-Kif2a knockdown or Es-Plk1 inhibition also affects these somatic cells requires further research of in vitro cell lines. 5. Conclusions In conclusion, our results showed that Es-KIF2A upregulates the PI3K/AKT signaling pathway through Es-PLK1 during E. sinensis spermatogenesis, thus affecting the prolifera- tion and apoptosis levels of spermatogenic cells. However, further research needs to be conducted to verify these results. Results and Discussion Truncated and mutated forms of ZFP809 proteins expressed from a lentiviral vector ZFP809 contains two major domains, one KRAB domain and a domain of seven zinc fingers. As a tool to identify functionally important sub-domains in ZFP809, we generated vector con- structs for intact ZFP809 proteins, together with nine types of truncated forms and a mutated form using the lentiviral vector pLVSIN/CMV/IRES/mCherry (S1A Fig). All forms of proteins are tagged with FLAG at the N-terminus. Proteins are forcedly expressed under human cyto- megalovirus (CMV) immediate early enhancer-promoter control in mammalian cells. A fluo- rescent protein, mCherry, is also expressed bicistronically under the control of the CMV promoter. The sub-domain structures of the nine types of truncated forms (referred to as ΔSD, ΔZF, ZF1, ZF1-2, ZF1-3, ZF1-4, ZF1-5, ZF1-6, and ΔKRAB_A) are depicted in Fig 1A. The mutated form of ZFP809 protein, referred to as mtKRAB, has amino acid substitutions at the 13th to 15th amino acid residues of the KRAB domain (E13/14A-W15A corresponding to E16/ 17A-W18A of ZNF268) (Fig 1A and S2 Fig). These amino acid substitutions have been shown to abolish the interaction between KRAB-ZFPs and KAP1 [18]. We confirmed protein expres- sion from the 11 lentiviral vector constructs for ZFP809 proteins shown in Fig 1A by trans- ducing 293FT cells with each of the vectors, followed by sorting mCherry expressing cells and Western blot analysis (Fig 1B). We used these vector constructs to assess the functionality of truncated/mutated forms of ZFP809 proteins in terms of nuclear localization, gene silencing efficiency, and binding ability to PBS. The KRAB domain and zinc fingers cooperatively control the nuclear localization pattern of ZFP809 A previous study has reported that both of the KRAB domain and zinc fingers are necessary for the nuclear localization of KRAB-ZFPs [11]. The intact ZFP809 protein was localized in the nucleus excluding the nucleoli (Fig 1C and S3A Fig), consistent with a pre- vious study showing that no KRAB-ZFPs were detected in purified nucleoli [19]. The truncated forms, ΔSD, ZF1-2, ZF1-3, ZF1-4, ZF1-5, and ZF1-6 behaved in the same way as the intact ZFP809. Among the truncated forms of ZFP809, ΔZF and ZF1 were mainly localized in the nucleus excluding the nucleoli, but were also localized in the cytoplasm (Fig 1C and S3A Fig). ΔKRAB_A and mtKRAB were localized in the nucleus in all cells examined. However, the localization of these proteins differed from that of intact ZFP809 and the other truncated ver- sions in that ΔKRAB_A and mtKRAB were localized in the nucleus including nucleoli in the majority (approximately 80% and 70%, respectively) of the cells examined (Fig 1C and S3A Fig). The localization of ΔKRAB_A and mtKRAB in nucleoli was suggested by the overlap in the staining by the nucleolus maker and the anti-FLAG antibody. The alteration in the nucleo- lar localization pattern of ZFP809 upon the truncation/mutation of its KRAB A box observed in this study is consistent with the previous report for KRAB-ZFPs other than ZFP809 [11]. These results suggest that the KRAB A box domain and the first and second zinc fingers are required for the proper nuclear localization of ZFP809. The KRAB domain contributes to the nuclear localization pattern of ZFP809 through its interaction with KAP1 Previous studies have demonstrated that the KRAB domain within KRAB-ZFPs interacts with KAP1 and reinforces nuclear localization, and that the amino acid substitutions (E16/17A-W18A of ZFP268) in the KRAB domain nullify its interaction with KAP1 [11,18]. Although ΔZF and ZF1 were localized in both the cytoplasm and the nucleus, these trun- cated forms also showed the same nuclear localization pattern as KAP1 (Fig 2A and S3B Fig). However, ΔKRAB_A and mtKRAB showed the same nuclear localization pattern as KAP1 only in approximately 30% and 40% respectively of the cells assessed (Fig 2A and S3B Fig).                         Guide

                        You do not type anything

                        Select the words in the Methods text, then press Use highlighted text. The exact words you selected are what gets recorded.

                        Typing is what this design removes. Given a sentence like the one below, one person types RIPA, another types the whole parenthesis, a third types the clause. All three read the paper correctly and disagree in the data. Selecting removes that entirely.

                        The cell total protein was isolated using RIPA (Applygen Technologies Inc., Beijing, China). Protease inhibitor (CWBIO, Shanghai, China) was added into the RIPA at a ratio of 1:100.

                        For lysis buffer there, mark just RIPA, the first time it appears.

                        How much to select

                        The shortest phrase that states the detail. Not the whole sentence when three words will do, and not the vendor, city or catalog number unless the question asks for them.

                        Every question shows a worked example under What to mark. Follow it rather than your instinct.

                        When a paper gives several

                        Many papers list a table of antibodies with eight RRIDs, eight catalog numbers and several vendors. Mark them all. Press Use highlighted text once per value and they stack up in a numbered list. Press Done, next when the list is complete.

                        If they sit in a contiguous table or a run-on list, selecting the whole block in one go is fine and much faster. You are marking where the evidence is, not counting.

                        The same value repeated does not need marking twice. A paper that uses 1:1000 for six antibodies has one dilution, not six.

                        If you stop before you have them all, tick 'There are more of these than I marked'. That box matters more than it looks: without it, someone who stopped at five of eight cannot be told apart from someone who found everything, and the three they skipped would count against the extractor as mistakes it did not make.

                        What counts

                        Judge only what is written. Not what the authors almost certainly did, and not what is standard practice in the field.

                        Judge the text shown. The page shows the Methods section, and sometimes figure legends or the full text where no Methods heading was found.                         The shaded sentences

                        The faint tint marks the western blot section: sentences that name the blot, the gel, the membrane or its detection. It is the same for every question on a paper. Blot section jumps between its blocks. Other assays are left untinted, but an item can still be stated elsewhere, for example in a figure legend.

                        The warmer marks are the sentences most likely to hold the answer to the open question, and Prev and Next step between them.

                        Both are search aids and nothing more. Fixed keyword rules pick them; they are never told the answer, they sometimes shade irrelevant sentences and sometimes miss the right one. Read around them, and judge only the text.

                        Membrane cutting disclosedA statement that the membrane was cut into strips or pieces before antibody incubation, so that targets were probed on separate pieces. Cropping the image for the figure does not count. Western blot only.
                        Mark: Mark the statement. In membranes were cut at the 50 kDa marker and probed separately mark the whole phrase. Nothing to mark in blots were cropped for clarity.
                        Secondary antibody host or targetThe host species of a secondary antibody, or the species it recognizes, such as goat anti-rabbit or anti-mouse IgG. The conjugate or vendor alone does not count, and neither does a primary antibody's host. Counts for an antibody used in any assay, not only the blot.
                        Mark: Mark the host and target together where they sit together. Mark each distinct combination once. In HRP-conjugated goat anti-rabbit IgG mark goat anti-rabbit.
                        Substrate incubation timeHow long the membrane was incubated in the detection substrate before imaging. An exposure or imaging time does not count. Western blot only.
                        Mark: Mark the time. In incubated with ECL for 1 min mark 1 min. Nothing to mark in an exposure time.
                        Secondary antibody vendorThe company that supplied a secondary antibody, including the maker of a kit that supplies it. 'All antibodies were from X' counts. Counts for an antibody used in any assay, not only the blot.
                        Mark: Mark every distinct company name. Leave out the city and country. A table can be marked as one block. In HRP-conjugated goat anti-rabbit IgG (Jackson ImmunoResearch) mark Jackson ImmunoResearch.
                        Quantification procedureHow the blot's bands were quantified beyond naming software: background subtraction, the measurement (integrated density, area under the peak), or how bands were selected. 'Densitometry was performed' does not count, and neither does normalization alone, which is its own item. Western blot only.
                        Mark: Mark the words that describe the procedure. In integrated density was measured after rolling-ball background subtraction mark the whole phrase.
                        Quantified band identifiedA statement of which band or bands of the blot were quantified, by size or form (the 44 kDa band, the cleaved form), or that additional bands were present and documented. Naming the target protein alone, or a band size given only to identify the protein, does not count. Western blot only.
                        Mark: Mark the words that identify the quantified band or the extra bands. In densitometry was carried out on the ~44 kDa band as well as on the smaller ~25 kDa band mark the whole phrase. Nothing to mark in PAR2 protein was quantified.
                        Antibody lot numberA lot or batch number for an antibody. A catalog number does not count. Counts for an antibody used in any assay, not only the blot.
                        Mark: Mark every distinct lot number. In (Abcam, ab1234, lot GR147853-1) mark GR147853-1.
                        Antibody concentration by massAn antibody working concentration in mass per volume (for example 0.5 ug/mL). A dilution ratio alone does not count. Counts for an antibody used in any assay, not only the blot.
                        Mark: Mark each distinct concentration once. In anti-p53 at 1 ug/mL mark 1 ug/mL.
                        Replicate definitionWhat a replicate of the blot was, with the unit named: independent cultures or passages, animals, patients, separate blots, or technical repeats of one lysate. 'Independent experiments' or 'biological replicates' with no unit named does not count, and neither does a count alone. Western blot only.
                        Mark: Mark the words that say what was replicated. In three independent cell cultures, each blotted once mark the whole phrase. Nothing to mark in representative of three independent experiments.
                        Membrane source or gradeThe supplier, product name or grade of the blot membrane, such as Immobilon-P (Millipore) or Amersham Hybond. Membrane chemistry or pore size alone does not count, and neither does the supplier of a transfer system or kit that does not name the membrane, or an Immobilon ECL substrate, which is the ECL reagent. Western blot only.
                        Mark: Mark the supplier or product words for the membrane. In transferred to PVDF membranes (Immobilon-P, Millipore) mark Immobilon-P, Millipore. Nothing to mark in transferred to PVDF membranes.
