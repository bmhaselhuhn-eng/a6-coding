What counts as a western blot. Western blot means proteins separated on a gel, transferred to a membrane and detected with an antibody. An OxyBlot counts. A dot blot or slot blot, of RNA or of protein, does not; neither does a capillary system such as Wes or Jess, which has no membrane, or a membrane probed only with streptavidin or a lectin, which uses no antibody. Items marked 'Western blot only' do not count the same thing described for another assay in the paper: qPCR, immunofluorescence, IHC, flow cytometry, ELISA, mass spectrometry, or a gel that was stained rather than blotted. Judge the text the page shows, including any figure legend printed in it; a detail found only in a legend or supplement that is not shown is not stated. A method given only by citation ('as previously described [12]') is not stated: mark only what the text itself says. The Shortcut citation item is the exception: it records the citation phrase itself. Some PDFs drop the micro sign or print it as m ('50 g', '25 mg' for micrograms); rule on the intended unit.

Every question carries its own What to mark rule with a worked example. Follow it rather than your instinct: consistency between reviewers matters more here than any single choice. Full rules under Guide.

Fruit variety selection: The meticulous choice of fruit varieties holds paramount significance. Kumquat fruits were identified as our preferred material for transient transformation due to their characteristics of thin skin and tender flesh (Figure 1A); additionally, they contain high sugar content and low acid, which provides a suitable growth environment for Agrobacterium. 'Densitometry was performed' does not count, and neither does normalization alone, which is its own item. Western blot only.
                        Mark: Mark the words that describe the procedure. In integrated density was measured after rolling-ball background subtraction mark the whole phrase.
                        Substrate incubation timeHow long the membrane was incubated in the detection substrate before imaging. An exposure or imaging time does not count. Western blot only.
                        Mark: Mark the time. In incubated with ECL for 1 min mark 1 min. Nothing to mark in an exposure time.
                        Shortcut citationA statement that the blot method, or a step of it, was done as described in a cited source ('as previously described [12]', 'according to Smith et al.'), with or without details given. A citation for a reagent, for an antibody's validation, or for another assay does not count, and neither does following a manufacturer's instructions. Western blot only.
                        Mark: Mark the phrase that points to the citation. In Western blotting was performed as previously described (24) mark as previously described (24). Nothing to mark in qPCR was performed as previously described (12).
                        Blot protocol depositedA statement that a detailed protocol for the blot is deposited in a protocol repository or protocol journal (protocols.io, Protocol Exchange, Bio-protocol), with its link or DOI. A citation to a research paper, a manufacturer's protocol, or a lab website does not count. Western blot only.
                        Mark: Mark the statement with its link or DOI. In the full protocol is available at protocols.io (dx.doi.org/10.17504/...) mark the whole phrase.
                        Software versionA version number for software used to acquire or quantify the blot. A software name alone, or a version of statistics software (Prism, SPSS), does not count. Western blot only.
                        Mark: Mark the version. In ImageJ (v1.53, NIH) mark v1.53.
                        Gel and running buffer systemThe gel chemistry or the running buffer, either one (Tris-glycine, Bis-Tris, MOPS or MES, Tris-acetate, Tricine), or a named precast gel product. 'SDS-PAGE' alone, or a gel percentage alone, does not count. Western blot only.
                        Mark: Mark the words naming the system or product. In NuPAGE 4-12% Bis-Tris gels in MES running buffer mark the whole phrase.
                        Replicate definitionWhat a replicate of the blot was, with the unit named: independent cultures or passages, animals, patients, separate blots, or technical repeats of one lysate. 'Independent experiments' or 'biological replicates' with no unit named does not count, and neither does a count alone. Western blot only.
                        Mark: Mark the words that say what was replicated. In three independent cell cultures, each blotted once mark the whole phrase. Nothing to mark in representative of three independent experiments.
                        Sample buffer compositionThe loading or sample buffer's recipe, or the product used. A recipe with at least one component and its concentration counts, and so does a product identified by brand name, vendor or catalog number. 'Laemmli buffer' or 'loading buffer' alone does not count. Western blot only.
                        Mark: Mark the recipe, or the product with what identifies it. In 4x NuPAGE LDS Sample Buffer mark NuPAGE LDS Sample Buffer.
                        Replicate handling in analysisHow technical replicates entered the blot's analysis: repeat lanes or blots of the same sample averaged or combined, values excluded by a stated rule, or gel or membrane treated as a blocking or random effect in the statistics. Averaging biological replicates into a mean, a replicate count or definition alone, or a statistics statement that names no assay does not count. Western blot only.
                        Mark: Mark the statement. In each lysate was run in duplicate lanes and the two values were averaged mark the whole phrase. Nothing to mark in data represent the average of three independent experiments.
                        Signal within dynamic range statedA statement that the blot's signal was checked to lie within the linear or dynamic range, or to be unsaturated: a loading or dilution series, an unsaturated-exposure check, or the imager's saturation indicator. 'Optimal exposure', or an exposure time alone, does not count. Western blot only.
                        Mark: Mark the statement. In exposures were chosen so that no band was saturated mark the whole phrase. Nothing to mark in the optimal exposure was used.
