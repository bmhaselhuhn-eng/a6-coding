What counts as a western blot. Western blot means proteins separated on a gel, transferred to a membrane and detected with an antibody. An OxyBlot counts. A dot blot or slot blot, of RNA or of protein, does not; neither does a capillary system such as Wes or Jess, which has no membrane, or a membrane probed only with streptavidin or a lectin, which uses no antibody. Items marked 'Western blot only' do not count the same thing described for another assay in the paper: qPCR, immunofluorescence, IHC, flow cytometry, ELISA, mass spectrometry, or a gel that was stained rather than blotted. Judge the text the page shows, including any figure legend printed in it; a detail found only in a legend or supplement that is not shown is not stated. A method given only by citation ('as previously described [12]') is not stated: mark only what the text itself says. The Shortcut citation item is the exception: it records the citation phrase itself. Some PDFs drop the micro sign or print it as m ('50 g', '25 mg' for micrograms); rule on the intended unit.

Every question carries its own What to mark rule with a worked example. Follow it rather than your instinct: consistency between reviewers matters more here than any single choice. Full rules under Guide.

Results and Discussion In this work, a new nanosystem formulation with the ability to specifically co-deliver two targeted therapy drugs and a DNA plasmid containing a tumor suppressor gene into HCC cells was developed. As illustrated in Figure 1, these nanosystems consisted of a core-shell nanostructure comprising a PLGA core complexed with DNA, through electrostatic interactions, coated with a lipid bilayer composed of EPOPC, DOPE, PHC, DSPE-PEG2000-amine and DSPE -PEG2000-GalNac. The production of these lipid/polymer hybrid nanoparticles (HNP) was carried out in two steps. In the first step, the polymeric core was produced with the biodegradable and biocompatible PLGA copolymer using the solvent emulsifica­ tion diffusion-evaporation method.39 In order to produce PLGA nanoparticles with a positive surface charge and reduced size, the influence of different combinations of DMAB and PVA surfactants on these parameters was evaluated. As illustrated in Figure S1A and B, it was found that as the quantity of DMAB increases, there is a reduction in the size and an enhancement in the surface charge of the PLGA nanoparticles, that is in line with what was expected, since the DMAB surfactant, in addition to be a positively charged surfactant, allows the formation of small nanoparticles.39 Based on the obtained data, the PLGA nanoparticles produced with 30 mg of DMAB and 175 mg of PVA were selected to develop the HNP, since in these conditions it was attained nanoparticles smaller than 100 nm (≅ 92.3 nm), with moderately positive zeta potential (≅ +19.2 mV). Adolescent Chronic Intermittent Ethanol Vapor Exposure Adolescent mice were given a daily injection of either pyrazole + saline (Air-control group, 1 mmol/kg) or pyrazole + ethanol (AIE group, 1 mmol/kg + 0.8 g/kg, respectively) to impair the metabolism of ethanol. Mice were placed back in their home cages where they were left undisturbed for 30 min. Home cages were then placed into chambers filled with either volatilized water or ethanol (20.3 ± 0.2 mg/L); airflow through the chambers was maintained at 5.5 L/min and volatilization was maintained at 1.5 L/min. After 16 h of continuous exposure, mice were removed from the chambers and returned to standard animal housing. Ethanol chamber exposure occurred from 1600-0800 the following day, which allowed for the reliable obtainment of blood ethanol concentrations in the range of 150-185 mg/dL. This protocol was run for two, 4-day cycles of 16-h in-chamber sessions and 8-h out-of-chamber sessions. The two sessions were separated by 3 days of no vapor exposure. Male and female mice were exposed in separate vapor chambers. For adult ethanol re-exposure, mice (P70-75) underwent a single alcohol vapor chamber session (16 h).                       The faint tint marks the western blot section: sentences that name the blot, the gel, the membrane or its detection. It is the same for every question on a paper. Blot section jumps between its blocks. Other assays are left untinted, but an item can still be stated elsewhere, for example in a figure legend.

                      The warmer marks are the sentences most likely to hold the answer to the open question, and Prev and Next step between them.

                      Both are search aids and nothing more. Fixed keyword rules pick them; they are never told the answer, they sometimes shade irrelevant sentences and sometimes miss the right one. Read around them, and judge only the text.
